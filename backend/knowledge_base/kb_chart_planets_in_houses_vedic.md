# The Nine Grahas in the Twelve Bhavas

Each entry describes a tendency for the planet in a house counted from the lagna (whole-sign). Read it alongside the planet's sign and dignity (see `kb_chart_planets_in_signs_dignity.md`), its aspects and conjunctions, the functional nature for the lagna (see `kb_chart_functional_nature_by_lagna.md`) and the running dasha. When birth time is unknown, house positions cannot be given; use the Moon-based reference (houses counted from the Moon) only with an explicit label.

General principles used here:
- Natural malefics (Sun, Mars, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu) tend to do well in the upachaya houses 3, 6, 10 and 11, where they convert friction into effort and achievement.
- Natural benefics (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury, waxing Moon) support the houses they occupy, especially kendras and trikonas; in dusthanas 6, 8 and 12 they ask for conscious handling.
- *Karako bhava nashaya*: a karaka in its own house (Jupiter in the 5th, Venus in the 7th, Mars in the 3rd, Moon in the 4th, Sun in the 9th and so on) can over-focus on that matter; this is a classical caution that many modern teachers read as "the matter becomes a central lesson".
- Planets are described as tendencies. A planet is not good or bad in a house in isolation.

## Sun in the houses

### Sun in the 1st house
Strong sense of self, leadership and vitality; pride and a need for recognition are the themes to balance.

### Sun in the 2nd house
Authority around family and finances; a dignified, sometimes forceful way of speaking; values inherited from the father line.

### Sun in the 3rd house
Courage, initiative and self-made effort; a competitive, enterprising spirit and often accomplished with siblings or peers.

### Sun in the 4th house
Strong attachment to home and the father's or authority figures' influence on early life; inner restlessness seeking a sense of belonging.

### Sun in the 5th house
Intelligent, creative and drawn to leadership in learning or the arts; strong pull toward children and mentoring.

### Sun in the 6th house
Capacity to overcome rivals and manage service, health and discipline; recognised for diligence.

### Sun in the 7th house
Seeks status and a prominent partner; partnerships may challenge the ego and teach cooperation.

### Sun in the 8th house
Interest in research, hidden matters and endurance through crisis; a theme of self-transformation.

### Sun in the 9th house
Respect for dharma, teachers and higher learning; an authoritative or principled worldview; relationship to the father is a key theme.

### Sun in the 10th house
Prominent public role, ambition and leadership in career; the Sun is naturally strong here (digbala).

### Sun in the 11th house
Gains through authority, institutions and influential networks; fulfilment of ambitions.

### Sun in the 12th house
Inner life, retreat, foreign lands or hidden service; ego dissolves into something larger; expenses on causes.

## Moon in the houses

### Moon in the 1st house
Sensitive, adaptive and emotionally expressive personality; mood is visible and appearance may change with the phase.

### Moon in the 2nd house
Emotional ties to family, food and speech; fluctuating income pattern with a nurturing way of speaking.

### Moon in the 3rd house
Curious, communicative and emotionally driven effort; close ties with siblings and neighbours; mental versatility.

### Moon in the 4th house
Strong emotional roots and attachment to mother and home; the Moon is naturally strong here (digbala), bringing inner peace when supported.

### Moon in the 5th house
Imaginative, emotional intelligence; love of creativity, children and romance; mind seeks meaning through feeling.

### Moon in the 6th house
Emotional involvement in service and health matters; worry-prone but caring; a theme of learning to manage stress.

### Moon in the 7th house
Emotionally invested in partnership; sensitivity to others and public mood; relationships shape the sense of security.

### Moon in the 8th house
Deep, intuitive and secretive emotional life; interest in psychology and the occult; changes that call for emotional resilience.

### Moon in the 9th house
Devout, reflective and drawn to travel and philosophy; emotional need for purpose; ties to mother's guidance.

### Moon in the 10th house
Public-facing career; emotional engagement with work, often in caring, hospitality or public service; reputation fluctuates like the Moon.

### Moon in the 11th house
Social, popular; gains through groups and women/community networks; emotional fulfilment from friends.

### Moon in the 12th house
Rich inner life, imagination and need for solitude; interest in foreign places or spiritual practice; sleep and rest are themes.

## Mars in the houses

### Mars in the 1st house
Energetic, assertive and courageous; athletic or active body; impulsiveness to be tempered (counted for Mangal Dosha).

### Mars in the 2nd house
Direct, forceful speech; earning through effort; strong family feelings that can turn argumentative (Mangal Dosha house).

### Mars in the 3rd house
Excellent placement for courage, stamina and initiative; competitive with siblings; strong drive to learn skills.

### Mars in the 4th house
Passion around home, property and roots; emotional intensity at home; classically tied to property interests (Mangal Dosha house).

### Mars in the 5th house
Competitive intelligence, sports and strategy; protective drive toward children; strong opinions in creative life.

### Mars in the 6th house
Strong placement: overcomes rivals, ill health and obstacles; disciplined, service-driven energy.

### Mars in the 7th house
Passionate, dynamic partnerships and business dealings; assertiveness in relationships calls for balance (Mangal Dosha house).

### Mars in the 8th house
Intense, investigative and resilient; courage in crises; interest in surgery, research or occult; care needed with accidents and anger (Mangal Dosha house).

### Mars in the 9th house
Dynamic, crusading approach to belief and learning; courage in pursuing ideals; may argue with teachers.

### Mars in the 10th house
Strong placement (digbala): ambition, drive and capacity for executive or technical careers; assertive public presence.

### Mars in the 11th house
Strong placement: gains through effort, competition and bold networks; achieving goals through action.

### Mars in the 12th house
Energy turned inward or abroad; hidden efforts, spending of energy, interest in solitary or spiritual discipline (Mangal Dosha house).

## Mercury in the houses

### Mercury in the 1st house
Quick, curious, communicative personality; youthful look; skilled learner and talker.

### Mercury in the 2nd house
Skilled speech, trade and money handling; a wit with words; family and education themes.

### Mercury in the 3rd house
Writing, media, communication and skilled hands; strong bond with siblings; much mental energy.

### Mercury in the 4th house
Educated home, interest in learning in the domestic setting; mental activity about property or roots; mind seeks calm.

### Mercury in the 5th house
Intellect, analysis and creative thinking; interest in teaching, games and advising; mental bond with children.

### Mercury in the 6th house
Analytical, detail-oriented in service, health and routine; skill at problem-solving; nervous energy to be balanced.

### Mercury in the 7th house
Communicative partnerships; business and negotiation skills; partner may be youthful or intellectual.

### Mercury in the 8th house
Research mind, interest in the hidden and psychology; skilled in investigation, finance of others.

### Mercury in the 9th house
Philosophical and learned mind; interest in teaching, publishing and long journeys; an open, discussion-driven faith.

### Mercury in the 10th house
Career in communication, commerce, analysis or technology; versatile professional skills.

### Mercury in the 11th house
Gains through networks, trade and communication; many contacts and intellectual friendships.

### Mercury in the 12th house
Imaginative, reflective mind; research abroad or in solitude; thoughts are private and may be hard to voice.

## Jupiter in the houses

### Jupiter in the 1st house
Optimistic, wise and generous bearing; strong sense of ethics; classic protective influence on the whole chart.

### Jupiter in the 2nd house
Prosperity themes, valued speech, family wisdom; steady accumulation and teaching ability.

### Jupiter in the 3rd house
Optimistic initiative and encouragement of siblings; learning through effort; Jupiter's expansive nature is less strong here.

### Jupiter in the 4th house
Happy home, emotional contentment, strong early education; comfort and a sense of inner peace.

### Jupiter in the 5th house
Strong placement: wisdom, children, creativity and spiritual merit (note the classical caution that Jupiter in the 5th may limit or delay some children themes; modern readers soften this).

### Jupiter in the 6th house
Helps in service and health, but themes of over-giving or health-related excess are possible; effort resolves issues.

### Jupiter in the 7th house
Supportive, generous partner and public goodwill; a wise or elder-like partner; strong counsel in business.

### Jupiter in the 8th house
Interest in philosophy, research and inheritance; protective in crises; long life themes in classical texts.

### Jupiter in the 9th house
Strong placement: dharma, fortune and good teachers; faith, learning and travel flourish.

### Jupiter in the 10th house
Respected position, ethical career, advisory or teaching roles; reputation for integrity.

### Jupiter in the 11th house
Strong gains, supportive friends and fulfilment of wishes.

### Jupiter in the 12th house
Spiritual inclination, generosity, hidden charity; foreign settings; expenses on good causes; moksha-oriented.

## Venus in the houses

### Venus in the 1st house
Charming, graceful, aesthetically-minded personality; love of comfort and relationships.

### Venus in the 2nd house
Attractive speech, interest in beautiful things, family comfort, income through the arts or luxury.

### Venus in the 3rd house
Artistic skills, creative communication and affectionate sibling bonds; pleasure in short journeys.

### Venus in the 4th house
Comfortable home, vehicles and a harmonious domestic life; emotionally warm; love of beauty at home.

### Venus in the 5th house
Romantic, creative and artistic; pleasure in children, culture and entertainment.

### Venus in the 6th house
Challenges in comfort and relationship through service demands; ability to harmonise conflicts; health and indulgence are themes.

### Venus in the 7th house
Strong placement for partnership: charm, attraction and cooperation; Venus is karaka of marriage here (a classical caution of 'over-focus on the matter').

### Venus in the 8th house
Interest in deep intimacy, shared resources, and hidden pleasures; transformation through love.

### Venus in the 9th house
Refined values, love of philosophy and arts; harmonious relationship with teachers and the father figure.

### Venus in the 10th house
Career in arts, beauty, hospitality, diplomacy; pleasant public image.

### Venus in the 11th house
Gains through arts, social circles and relationships; fulfilment of desires.

### Venus in the 12th house
Strong placement: private pleasures, foreign or imaginative pursuits, sound sleep and spiritual surrender.

## Saturn in the houses

### Saturn in the 1st house
Serious, disciplined, reserved personality; matures with age; carries early responsibility.

### Saturn in the 2nd house
Careful with money and words; steady, slowly building resources; family responsibility.

### Saturn in the 3rd house
Strong placement: perseverance, stamina and disciplined effort; skills mastered slowly but thoroughly.

### Saturn in the 4th house
Inner seriousness, reserved home life, or responsibility for family; stable but slow comfort; learning patience.

### Saturn in the 5th house
Disciplined, structured thinker; slow or serious approach to creativity and children; mentoring responsibility.

### Saturn in the 6th house
Strong placement: disciplined service, ability to endure and defeat obstacles; long-term health through routine.

### Saturn in the 7th house
Serious, committed and sometimes delayed partnerships; mature partner; Saturn is strong here (digbala) in practical dealings.

### Saturn in the 8th house
Resilient, long-lived tendencies in classical texts; patient research; endurance through crises.

### Saturn in the 9th house
Conservative, disciplined approach to belief and learning; late-blooming wisdom; relationship with father/mentor is a lesson.

### Saturn in the 10th house
Strong placement: steady, hard-working career; authority built over time; responsibility and rank.

### Saturn in the 11th house
Strong placement: steady gains, loyal networks and goals achieved through persistence.

### Saturn in the 12th house
Solitude, discipline in retreat, foreign work or spiritual austerity; expenses are measured; sleep and rest are lessons.

## Rahu in the houses

### Rahu in the 1st house
Unconventional, ambitious personality; strong drive for identity; magnetism and a sense of 'difference'.

### Rahu in the 2nd house
Unusual family or financial background; unconventional speech; money drives and appetites to balance.

### Rahu in the 3rd house
Courageous, innovative effort; bold communication; success through technology or media.

### Rahu in the 4th house
Restlessness around home, roots or mother; living far from birthplace; seeking peace.

### Rahu in the 5th house
Innovative, speculative mind; unconventional creativity; themes around children or romance to approach mindfully.

### Rahu in the 6th house
Strong placement: overcomes opponents; skill in unconventional service; health through discipline.

### Rahu in the 7th house
Unusual or foreign partnerships; strong attraction and learning in relationships; need for clarity.

### Rahu in the 8th house
Fascination with mystery, research, occult; sudden changes; transformation.

### Rahu in the 9th house
Unorthodox beliefs, foreign teachers and long journeys; questioning of tradition.

### Rahu in the 10th house
Ambition, unconventional career, foreign or tech sectors; fame-seeking tendencies.

### Rahu in the 11th house
Strong placement: gains through networks, technology, large groups; fulfilment of desires.

### Rahu in the 12th house
Foreign residence, spiritual seeking, hidden pursuits; imagination and escapism to watch.

## Ketu in the houses

### Ketu in the 1st house
Detached, introspective personality; sense of mystery; independence; not easily labeled.

### Ketu in the 2nd house
Detached from money and family; unconventional speech; may be spiritual about resources.

### Ketu in the 3rd house
Courage with detachment; self-taught skills; strong intuition; distant sibling relations.

### Ketu in the 4th house
Inner detachment from home or comfort; spiritual attitude to roots; seeking inner peace.

### Ketu in the 5th house
Intuitive intelligence; spiritual or past-life oriented creativity; non-attachment around children.

### Ketu in the 6th house
Strong placement: overcomes opponents quietly; healing and service; detachment from conflict.

### Ketu in the 7th house
Detached or spiritual approach to partnership; learning about independence; karmic partnerships.

### Ketu in the 8th house
Mystical, occult and moksha-oriented interests; deep intuition; transformation.

### Ketu in the 9th house
Spiritual, non-dogmatic outlook; detachment from tradition; self-realisation.

### Ketu in the 10th house
Detached from status; unusual career, research or spiritual vocation.

### Ketu in the 11th house
Gains by non-attachment; small but meaningful networks; fulfilment not tied to ambition.

### Ketu in the 12th house
Strong placement: moksha-oriented; retreat, meditation and letting go.
