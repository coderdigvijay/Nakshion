# Karakas, Arudha and Karakamsa

## Naisargika (natural) karakas

A karaka is a significator: a planet naturally associated with a theme. Reading a house means looking at the house, its lord and its karaka together.

| House | Primary karaka(s) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sun | self, vitality; Moon for mind, Mars for energy in some lists |
| 2 | Jupiter | wealth and family; Mercury for speech, Venus for food in some lists |
| 3 | Mars | courage, siblings, effort; Mercury for communication |
| 4 | Moon | mother, home, peace of mind; Mercury (education), Venus (comforts, vehicles) |
| 5 | Jupiter | children, intelligence, merit |
| 6 | Mars and Saturn | health challenges, service, opponents; Mars for conflicts, Saturn for chronic matters |
| 7 | Venus | marriage and partnership; Jupiter for the spouse of women's charts in some texts |
| 8 | Saturn | longevity, transformation; Mars as secondary |
| 9 | Jupiter and Sun | dharma, mentors, father (Sun) |
| 10 | Sun, Mercury, Jupiter, Saturn | authority (Sun), business (Mercury), status (Jupiter), work (Saturn) |
| 11 | Jupiter | gains, networks; Mercury and Saturn in some lists |
| 12 | Saturn | loss, seclusion; Ketu for liberation, Venus for bed pleasures |

BPHS gives a different list in places, for example Mercury for friends, Venus for conveyances, Saturn for servants. Lists are consistent on the core assignments (Sun-father, Moon-mother, Mars-siblings, Mercury-intelligence, Jupiter-children, Venus-spouse, Saturn-longevity).

An important Parashari principle: *karako bhava nashaya* — a karaka in the house it signifies may weaken that house's matters, a nuance especially cited for Jupiter in the 5th, Venus in the 7th, Mars in the 3rd. Many modern teachers treat it as "may overdo or over-focus on that matter", and note it is not universal.

## Jaimini chara (variable) karakas

In Jaimini's system, significators are assigned by relative degree rather than fixed by nature. Take each planet's degree within its sign (ignoring the sign) and rank from highest to lowest.

| Rank by degree | Karaka | Signifies |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (highest) | Atmakaraka (AK) | soul, core self, life lesson |
| 2 | Amatyakaraka (AmK) | career, advisor, mind-in-action |
| 3 | Bhratrikaraka (BK) | siblings, courage |
| 4 | Matrikaraka (MK) | mother, nurturing, education |
| 5 | Pitrikaraka (PiK) | father |
| 6 | Putrakaraka (PK) | children, creativity |
| 7 | Gnatikaraka (GK) | relatives, obstacles, competition |
| 8 (lowest) | Darakaraka (DK) | spouse, partnership |

Note: some texts omit Pitrikaraka in the seven-karaka version, so that the Putrakaraka falls at rank 5 [school difference]. The eight-karaka version includes Rahu, whose degree is measured backward (30 minus its degree in the sign), because Rahu moves in reverse. The seven-karaka version uses the Sun to Saturn only and brings in Rahu when two planets share the same degree. Ketu is not given a karaka role. This count (7 vs 8) is a standing debate among Jaimini scholars; the app, if it uses chara karakas, should state which it uses.

**Practical reading.** The AK is the "planet of the soul". Its sign, house and condition describe what the person is working through; a strong AK shows a purposeful orientation, a challenged AK shows where lessons are most intense. The DK describes the qualities of the spouse or partner; the AmK describes the working life. These are tendencies to explore, not fixed identities.

## Arudha Lagna (AL) and Arudha padas

The arudha (pada) of a house is its "image": how the matters of that house appear in the world, as opposed to the house itself. The calculation:

1. Find the house lord and count how many signs it is from the house (inclusive count, so the house itself counts as the first).
2. Count the same number of signs forward from the lord's sign. The sign reached is the arudha (pada) of the house.
3. Exceptions: if the arudha falls in the same house (1st from itself) or in the 7th from it, take the 10th from that house instead. Result: the arudha is never in the house itself or its opposite.

Applied to the 1st house this gives the **Arudha Lagna (AL)**, also called Pada Lagna. It reflects public image, reputation and how the person is perceived. Applied to the 12th house it gives the **Upapada Lagna (UL)**, which Jaimini uses for marriage, the spouse's nature and the maintenance of the household. Other padas: A2 (Dhana pada, wealth image), A3, A4 (property), A5, A6 (opponents), A7 (Darapada), A8, A9, A10 (Rajya pada, career image), A11 (Labha pada), A12 (the UL).

How to use it: planets in or aspecting the AL colour public perception; the 2nd house from the UL and its lord are considered for the marriage's longevity; the 7th from the AL is also used for partnerships. As with every Jaimini technique, results depend on a reliable birth time because the lagna and house lords change with the lagna sign.

## Karakamsa

The sign occupied by the Atmakaraka in the navamsa (D9) is called Karakamsa. When written on the rashi chart it forms a second reference lagna. Jaimini uses it for reading spiritual path, profession and "soul purpose". Planets in the Karakamsa, in the 2nd, 5th, 9th, 10th and 12th from it, and the Atmakaraka's aspects are read for tendencies toward arts, learning, health, spirituality and so on. Swamsa is another name for the Karakamsa. A common (but only partly sourced) rule of thumb treats natural benefics on the Karakamsa as supportive and natural malefics as challenging [unverified in the primary text].

Example: if the AK is Jupiter, and Jupiter lies in Taurus in D1 and in Pisces in D9, the Karakamsa is Pisces. An example reading: Pisces Karakamsa with Venus nearby may point to a creative or devotional orientation; this is an inference and should be marked as tendency.

## Data requirements

- Chara karakas need only planetary longitudes, so they can be computed without birth time (the Moon's degree depends on time of day, so the ranking may be ambiguous; see the unknown-time notes).
- Arudha, Upapada and Karakamsa need the lagna (arudha) and the D9 chart; they should not be stated when birth time is unknown.
- The engine does not currently compute chara karakas, arudhas or Karakamsa; an assistant should explain them conceptually and not invent positions.
