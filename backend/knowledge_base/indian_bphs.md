# Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra — Deep Dive into Foundational Vedic Astrology

## Overview

The Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (BPHS) is the foundational text of Vedic (Jyotish) astrology, attributed to the sage Parashara. It is the most comprehensive classical text on horoscopic astrology in the Indian tradition, covering planetary characteristics, house significations, dasha systems, yogas, divisional charts, and remedial measures. Virtually every technique in modern Vedic astrology traces back to BPHS. The text consists of approximately 4,000 verses organized into nearly 100 chapters.

## Graha (Planetary) Characteristics in Detail

### The Nine Grahas

Vedic astrology uses the seven classical planets plus two shadow planets (lunar nodes):

**Surya (Sun)**
- Nature: Malefic (krura), masculine, sattvic
- Element: Fire (agni tattva)
- Dosha: Pitta
- Caste: Kshatriya (warrior)
- Gem: Ruby (manikya)
- Metal: Gold (suvarna)
- Direction: East
- Body parts: Bones, heart, right eye, stomach
- Significations: Soul (atma), father, authority, government, vitality, ego, kingship, honor
- Strong placement: 10th house, Aries exaltation (10 degrees), Leo domicile
- Weak placement: Libra (debilitation at 10 degrees), Aquarius detriment, 7th house

**Chandra (Moon)**
- Nature: Benefic when waxing (shukla paksha), malefic when waning (krishna paksha); feminine, sattvic
- Element: Water (jala tattva)
- Dosha: Vata-Kapha
- Caste: Vaishya (merchant)
- Gem: Pearl (moti) or moonstone
- Metal: Silver
- Direction: Northwest
- Body parts: Blood, breasts, mind, left eye, lungs, fluids
- Significations: Mind (manas), mother, emotions, public, nourishment, fertility, travel
- Strong: 4th house, Taurus exaltation (3 degrees), Cancer domicile
- Weak: Scorpio debilitation (3 degrees), Capricorn detriment

**Mangala (Mars)**
- Nature: Malefic, masculine, tamasic
- Element: Fire
- Dosha: Pitta
- Caste: Kshatriya
- Gem: Red coral (moonga)
- Metal: Copper
- Direction: South
- Body parts: Muscle, blood, bone marrow, head, genitals
- Significations: Energy (virya), siblings, courage, land, property, surgery, accidents, military, sports
- Strong: 10th house, Capricorn exaltation (28 degrees), Aries and Scorpio domicile
- Weak: Cancer debilitation (28 degrees), Libra and Taurus detriment

**Budha (Mercury)**
- Nature: Benefic when alone or with benefics, malefic with malefics; neutral gender, rajasic
- Element: Earth (prithvi tattva)
- Dosha: Tridoshic (all three)
- Caste: Vaishya
- Gem: Emerald (panna)
- Metal: Bronze/alloy
- Direction: North
- Body parts: Skin, nervous system, speech organs, hands, intestines
- Significations: Intelligence (buddhi), communication, commerce, education, mathematics, writing, humor
- Strong: 1st house, Virgo exaltation (15 degrees), Gemini and Virgo domicile
- Weak: Pisces debilitation (15 degrees), Sagittarius detriment

**Guru (Jupiter)**
- Nature: Benefic (the greatest benefic), masculine, sattvic
- Element: Ether/Space (akasha tattva)
- Dosha: Kapha
- Caste: Brahmana (priest/scholar)
- Gem: Yellow sapphire (pukhraj)
- Metal: Gold
- Direction: Northeast
- Body parts: Liver, fat, brain, ears, thighs
- Significations: Wisdom (jnana), guru/teacher, children, wealth, dharma, religion, expansion, optimism, husband (for women)
- Strong: 1st house, Cancer exaltation (5 degrees), Sagittarius and Pisces domicile
- Weak: Capricorn debilitation (5 degrees), Gemini and Virgo detriment

**Shukra (Venus)**
- Nature: Benefic, feminine, rajasic
- Element: Water
- Dosha: Kapha-Vata
- Caste: Brahmana
- Gem: Diamond (heera)
- Metal: Silver
- Direction: Southeast
- Body parts: Reproductive system, face, eyes, kidneys, skin lustre
- Significations: Love (kama), wife (for men), luxury, art, music, vehicles, beauty, comforts, sensuality
- Strong: 4th house, Pisces exaltation (27 degrees), Taurus and Libra domicile
- Weak: Virgo debilitation (27 degrees), Aries and Scorpio detriment

**Shani (Saturn)**
- Nature: Malefic (greatest malefic), neutral gender, tamasic
- Element: Air (vayu tattva)
- Dosha: Vata
- Caste: Shudra (laborer)
- Gem: Blue sapphire (neelam)
- Metal: Iron
- Direction: West
- Body parts: Legs, joints, teeth, nerves, chronic diseases
- Significations: Longevity (ayus), karma, discipline, delay, separation, servants, sorrow, old age, detachment, renunciation
- Strong: 7th house, Libra exaltation (20 degrees), Capricorn and Aquarius domicile
- Weak: Aries debilitation (20 degrees), Cancer and Leo detriment

**Rahu (North Node)**
- Nature: Malefic, tamasic, acts like Saturn
- Gem: Hessonite garnet (gomed)
- Significations: Obsession, foreign things, unconventional paths, illusion, amplification, worldly desire, outcaste activities, sudden gains/losses, technology, foreigners
- Exaltation: Taurus or Gemini (varies by text)
- Owns no sign but co-rules Aquarius in some traditions

**Ketu (South Node)**
- Nature: Malefic, tamasic, acts like Mars
- Gem: Cat's eye (lehsunia/vaidurya)
- Significations: Liberation (moksha), spirituality, detachment, past-life karma, loss, isolation, mysticism, occult, surgical precision, monks
- Exaltation: Scorpio or Sagittarius (varies by text)
- Owns no sign but co-rules Scorpio in some traditions

## Bhava (House) Significations

BPHS provides extremely detailed house meanings:

| Bhava | Sanskrit Name | Core Significations |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | Lagna/Tanu | Body, self, personality, health, longevity, appearance, head |
| 2nd | Dhana | Wealth, family, speech, food/diet, right eye, face, early education |
| 3rd | Sahaja | Siblings, courage, short travel, communication, hands, arms, ears |
| 4th | Sukha | Mother, happiness, home, property, vehicles, education, heart, chest |
| 5th | Putra | Children, intelligence, creativity, past-life merit (purva punya), mantra, stomach |
| 6th | Ripu | Enemies, disease, debt, service, competition, maternal uncle, obstacles |
| 7th | Kalatra | Spouse, partnerships, business, foreign travel, death (maraka), lower abdomen |
| 8th | Ayu | Longevity, death, occult, transformation, inheritance, chronic disease, research |
| 9th | Dharma | Father, fortune, religion, guru, higher learning, long travel, law, hips |
| 10th | Karma | Career, status, authority, government, karma, knees |
| 11th | Labha | Gains, income, elder siblings, friends, hopes, fulfillment of desires |
| 12th | Vyaya | Loss, expenditure, foreign lands, spiritual liberation, sleep, bed pleasures, feet |

### House Classifications

- **Kendra (angular):** 1, 4, 7, 10 — pillars of the chart, Vishnu sthana
- **Trikona (trine):** 1, 5, 9 — most auspicious, Lakshmi sthana
- **Upachaya (growing):** 3, 6, 10, 11 — improve with time, malefics do well here
- **Dusthana (difficult):** 6, 8, 12 — houses of suffering and loss
- **Maraka (death-inflicting):** 2, 7 — their lords can trigger death or health crises in dashas
- **Trishadaya:** 3, 6, 11 — houses of effort and growth (a subset of the upachaya houses; the kama/desire trine is 3, 7, 11)

### Yoga Karaka Concept

A planet ruling both a kendra and a trikona becomes a Yoga Karaka — the single most beneficial planet for that ascendant. Examples:
- For Taurus Lagna: Saturn rules 9th (trikona) and 10th (kendra) — supreme benefic
- For Libra Lagna: Saturn rules 4th (kendra) and 5th (trikona) — supreme benefic
- For Cancer Lagna: Mars rules 5th (trikona) and 10th (kendra) — supreme benefic
- For Leo Lagna: Mars rules 4th and 9th — supreme benefic

## Dasha Systems

### Vimshottari Dasha (120-year cycle)

The most widely used timing system in Vedic astrology. Based on the Moon's nakshatra at birth:

| Planet | Duration (years) | Starting Nakshatra |
|---|---|---|
| Ketu | 7 | Ashwini, Magha, Mula |
| Venus | 20 | Bharani, Purva Phalguni, Purva Ashadha |
| Sun | 6 | Krittika, Uttara Phalguni, Uttara Ashadha |
| Moon | 10 | Rohini, Hasta, Shravana |
| Mars | 7 | Mrigashira, Chitra, Dhanishta |
| Rahu | 18 | Ardra, Swati, Shatabhisha |
| Jupiter | 16 | Punarvasu, Vishakha, Purva Bhadrapada |
| Saturn | 19 | Pushya, Anuradha, Uttara Bhadrapada |
| Mercury | 17 | Ashlesha, Jyeshtha, Revati |

**Calculating the starting dasha:** The Moon's exact longitude within its nakshatra determines how much of the first dasha period remains at birth. If Moon is exactly at the start of Rohini, the full 10-year Moon dasha operates from birth. If Moon is halfway through Rohini, only 5 years of Moon dasha remain.

**Sub-periods (Bhukti/Antardasha):** Each major period is subdivided among all nine planets in the same sequence. Venus dasha (20 years) begins with Venus-Venus sub-period, then Venus-Sun, Venus-Moon, and so on. Sub-periods are further divided into pratyantardasha (sub-sub-periods) for precise timing.

### Ashtottari Dasha (108-year cycle)

An alternative system used when Rahu is in a kendra or trikona:

| Planet | Duration |
|---|---|
| Sun | 6 |
| Moon | 15 |
| Mars | 8 |
| Mercury | 17 |
| Saturn | 10 |
| Jupiter | 19 |
| Rahu | 12 |
| Venus | 21 |

Note: Ketu is excluded in Ashtottari.

### Interpreting Dasha Results

A dasha planet delivers results based on:
1. Its natural significations (Sun = authority, Moon = mind, etc.)
2. The houses it rules in the birth chart
3. The house it occupies
4. The planets it associates with (conjunction/aspect)
5. Its strength (dignity, shadbala, avastha)
6. Its relationship with the sub-period lord

**Example:** If Jupiter rules the 2nd (wealth) and 5th (children, intelligence) houses and sits in the 11th (gains), Jupiter dasha brings wealth, children, educational success, and fulfillment of desires. If Jupiter is debilitated in Capricorn and aspected by Saturn, the same dasha brings these significations but with delays, obstacles, and the need for extra effort.

## Yoga Formations

Yogas are specific planetary combinations that produce defined results. BPHS catalogues hundreds. Key categories:

### Pancha Mahapurusha Yogas (Five Great Person Yogas)

Formed when a non-luminary planet is in its own sign or exaltation AND in a kendra (1, 4, 7, 10):

| Yoga | Planet | Results |
|---|---|---|
| Ruchaka | Mars | Courageous, commanding, military/athletic success, red complexion |
| Bhadra | Mercury | Eloquent, learned, skilled in commerce, youthful appearance |
| Hamsa | Jupiter | Virtuous, wise, devoted, wealthy, respected, fair complexion |
| Malavya | Venus | Beautiful, luxurious, artistic, wealthy, charismatic, amorous |
| Shasha | Saturn | Powerful authority, disciplined, commands servants, lean body |

### Dhana Yogas (Wealth Combinations)

- Lords of 1st and 2nd in mutual aspect or conjunction
- Lords of 5th and 9th connected (Lakshmi yoga)
- Lord of 11th in kendra with lord of 2nd
- Jupiter in 2nd or 11th unafflicted
- Venus and Jupiter in mutual kendras

### Raja Yogas (Power/Status Combinations)

- Lord of a kendra conjoins lord of a trikona
- Lord of 9th and 10th in conjunction, mutual aspect, or exchange
- Lord of 1st in 4th or 10th aspected by lord of 9th
- Multiple planets in kendras (especially 1st and 10th)

### Significant Named Yogas

**Gajakesari Yoga:** Jupiter in kendra from Moon. Gives fame, intelligence, wealth, and lasting reputation. One of the most common and beneficial yogas.

**Budhaditya Yoga:** Sun and Mercury conjunct (extremely common — Mercury never strays far from Sun). Gives intelligence and communication skill. Strongest when Mercury is not combust (more than 14 degrees from Sun) and in a good sign.

**Chandra-Mangala Yoga:** Moon and Mars conjunct or in mutual aspect. Gives wealth through enterprising ventures. Can also indicate emotional volatility.

**Neecha Bhanga Raja Yoga:** Cancellation of debilitation — a debilitated planet whose debilitation is cancelled by specific conditions (lord of the debilitation sign in kendra, lord of the exaltation sign in kendra from Moon or Lagna). Produces extraordinary results after initial struggle.

**Viparita Raja Yoga:** Lords of dusthanas (6, 8, 12) placed in other dusthanas. Loss of enemies, debts, and obstacles creates gain. Powerful for overcoming adversity.

**Kala Sarpa Yoga:** All seven planets hemmed between Rahu and Ketu. Controversial — some consider it severely obstructive, others note many highly successful people have it. Generally indicates karmic intensity and a life of extremes.

## Divisional Charts (Varga Charts)

BPHS prescribes 16 primary divisional charts, each revealing a specific life domain in greater detail:

| Chart | Division | Domain |
|---|---|---|
| D-1 (Rashi) | Main chart | Overall life |
| D-2 (Hora) | 2 divisions per sign | Wealth |
| D-3 (Drekkana) | 3 per sign | Siblings, courage |
| D-4 (Chaturthamsha) | 4 per sign | Property, fortune |
| D-7 (Saptamsha) | 7 per sign | Children, progeny |
| D-9 (Navamsha) | 9 per sign | Spouse, dharma, soul purpose |
| D-10 (Dashamsha) | 10 per sign | Career, profession |
| D-12 (Dwadashamsha) | 12 per sign | Parents |
| D-16 (Shodashamsha) | 16 per sign | Vehicles, comforts |
| D-20 (Vimshamsha) | 20 per sign | Spiritual life |
| D-24 (Chaturvimshamsha) | 24 per sign | Education, learning |
| D-27 (Bhamsha) | 27 per sign | Strengths, weaknesses |
| D-30 (Trimshamsha) | 30 per sign | Evils, misfortunes |
| D-40 (Khavedamsha) | 40 per sign | Auspicious/inauspicious effects |
| D-45 (Akshavedamsha) | 45 per sign | General indications |
| D-60 (Shashtiamsha) | 60 per sign | Past-life karma, everything |

### The Navamsha (D-9): The Most Important Divisional Chart

The Navamsha is consulted alongside the Rashi (D-1) for every interpretation. It reveals:

- **Spouse characteristics:** Planets in the 7th house of Navamsha describe the spouse. The Navamsha sign of the 7th lord indicates spouse qualities.
- **Strength confirmation:** A planet in its own sign or exaltation in Navamsha (Vargottama if same sign in both D-1 and D-9) is extremely strong.
- **Dharma and soul purpose:** The Navamsha Lagna and its lord reveal the person's deeper spiritual orientation.
- **Maturation of promise:** What the D-1 promises, the D-9 confirms or denies. A Raja Yoga in D-1 that is not supported in D-9 will underperform.

## Shadbala (Six-Fold Strength)

BPHS provides a quantitative system for assessing planetary strength across six dimensions, measured in units called rupas (1 rupa = 60 virupas):

### The Six Components

1. **Sthana Bala (Positional Strength):** Based on sign placement — own sign, exaltation, friendly sign, etc. Also includes uchcha bala (exaltation strength, maximum at exact exaltation degree), saptavargaja bala (strength across seven divisional charts), and ojhayugmarasyamsha bala (odd/even sign placement).

2. **Dig Bala (Directional Strength):** Each planet gains maximum strength in a specific house:
   - Jupiter and Mercury strongest in 1st house (East)
   - Sun and Mars strongest in 10th house (South)
   - Saturn strongest in 7th house (West)
   - Moon and Venus strongest in 4th house (North)

3. **Kala Bala (Temporal Strength):** Based on time factors — day/night strength (diurnal planets strong by day, nocturnal by night), monthly strength (based on lunar phase), yearly strength, hourly strength (planetary hours), and ayanamsha bala.

4. **Cheshta Bala (Motional Strength):** Based on planetary speed and direction. Retrograde planets gain cheshta bala. Maximum for a planet at its stationary retrograde point; minimum at its fastest direct motion.

5. **Naisargika Bala (Natural Strength):** Fixed values based on inherent luminosity: Sun (60), Moon (51.43), Venus (42.86), Jupiter (34.29), Mercury (25.71), Mars (17.14), Saturn (8.57).

6. **Drik Bala (Aspectual Strength):** Strength gained or lost through aspects from benefics (positive) or malefics (negative).

### Required Minimum Shadbala

| Planet | Minimum (in rupas) |
|---|---|
| Sun | 5.0 |
| Moon | 6.0 |
| Mars | 5.0 |
| Mercury | 7.0 |
| Jupiter | 6.5 |
| Venus | 5.5 |
| Saturn | 5.0 |

A planet scoring above its minimum can deliver its results effectively. Below the minimum, it struggles and its significations suffer.

## Ashtakavarga System

A unique Vedic technique for assessing house strength using a point-based system. Each of the seven planets (plus the Lagna) contributes benefic points (bindus) to each of the twelve houses.

### How It Works

Each planet distributes positive points across the twelve houses based on fixed rules. For example, the Sun gives benefic points in houses 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 counted from itself, and different positions counted from other planets.

### Key Thresholds

- A house with **28 or more** bindus in Sarvashtakavarga (total from all planets) produces favorable results when transited or during related dashas.
- A house with **25-28** bindus gives average results.
- A house with **below 25** bindus indicates difficulty in that area of life.
- Maximum possible per house: 56 bindus. Minimum: 0.

### Practical Application

Ashtakavarga is primarily used for:
1. **Transit analysis:** A planet transiting a sign where it has high bindus in its own ashtakavarga gives good results during that transit.
2. **Longevity calculation:** Specific techniques using Ashtakavarga points to estimate lifespan.
3. **Financial prediction:** The 11th house Sarvashtakavarga score indicates overall wealth potential.

## Planetary Avasthas (States)

BPHS describes several systems of planetary states that refine interpretation beyond dignity and strength:

### Baladi Avasthas (Age States)

Each planet is assigned an age based on its degree within the sign:

| Degree Range | Avastha | Strength | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-6 | Bala (infant) | Very weak | New, immature, cannot act independently |
| 6-12 | Kumara (adolescent) | Moderate | Growing but not yet capable |
| 12-18 | Yuva (young adult) | Full strength | Peak vitality and capacity |
| 18-24 | Vriddha (mature) | Declining | Experienced but losing vigor |
| 24-30 | Mrita (dead) | Very weak | Exhausted, unable to produce results |

Note: For odd signs the sequence progresses as listed. For even signs the sequence reverses (0-6 = Mrita, 24-30 = Bala). A planet in the Yuva (young adult) state delivers its best results regardless of other factors.

### Jagradadi Avasthas (Waking States)

- **Jagrat (Awake):** Planet in its own sign or exaltation — fully conscious and productive
- **Swapna (Dreaming):** Planet in a friendly sign — partially effective, results come but with effort
- **Sushupta (Sleeping):** Planet in an enemy sign or debilitation — inactive, results delayed or denied

### Lajjitadi Avasthas (Emotional States)

These describe more nuanced psychological states based on planetary combinations:

- **Lajjita (Ashamed):** Planet in the 5th house conjoined with Rahu, Ketu, Saturn, or Mars — creative expression is inhibited by shame or fear
- **Garvita (Proud):** Planet in exaltation or own sign — confidence, self-assurance, effectiveness
- **Kshudita (Hungry):** Planet in an enemy sign or conjoined with an enemy — restless craving, unsatisfied desire
- **Trushita (Thirsty):** Planet in a water sign aspected by an enemy and not aspected by a friend — emotional neediness, spiritual longing
- **Mudita (Happy):** Planet in a friendly sign and conjoined with or aspected by Jupiter — contentment, beneficence, easy expression
- **Kshobhita (Agitated):** Planet conjoined with the Sun and aspected by or conjoined with a malefic — disturbed, combative, anxious

### Deeptadi Avasthas (Luminosity States)

Based on the planet's proximity to its exaltation and debilitation degrees:

- **Deepta (Blazing):** At exact exaltation — maximum luminosity and power
- **Swastha (Comfortable):** In own sign — healthy and self-sufficient
- **Mudita (Delighted):** In a friend's sign — happy and cooperative
- **Shanta (Peaceful):** In auspicious vargas — serene effectiveness
- **Vikala (Crippled):** Combust — impaired function
- **Khala (Wicked):** In debilitation — produces results through negative means
- **Dukhita (Miserable):** In an enemy sign — suffers and causes suffering

## Graha Drishti (Planetary Aspects) in BPHS

Unlike Western astrology which uses degree-based aspects, Vedic aspects are cast from sign to sign with specific rules:

### All Planets

Every planet aspects the 7th house (sign) from itself with full strength.

### Special Aspects

- **Mars:** Additional aspects on the 4th and 8th houses from itself (full strength)
- **Jupiter:** Additional aspects on the 5th and 9th houses from itself (full strength)
- **Saturn:** Additional aspects on the 3rd and 10th houses from itself (full strength)
- **Rahu and Ketu:** Follow the same aspects as the planets they are conjoined with, or aspect the 5th and 9th (some traditions)

### Aspect Strength

In BPHS, aspects are either full (100%) or partial. The 7th aspect is always full. The special aspects above (Mars 4/8, Jupiter 5/9, Saturn 3/10) are full strength for that planet. In the graded (partial) scheme used for aspect-strength calculations, every planet casts: 3rd/10th = 25%, 5th/9th = 50%, 4th/8th = 75%, 7th = 100% (quarter-aspect rule).

### Practical Significance

A planet aspects an entire sign, not a specific degree. Saturn in Aries aspects Libra (7th), Gemini (3rd from Aries), and Capricorn (10th from Aries) with full strength. Every planet in those signs receives Saturn's influence regardless of their exact degree.

This system creates a denser aspect network than Western astrology. Most charts have every planet aspected by at least two other planets, creating a web of mutual influence that must be evaluated holistically.

The BPHS system is extraordinarily comprehensive and internally consistent. Mastering even its major techniques — dashas, yogas, divisional charts, avasthas, and Shadbala — provides a practitioner with powerful tools for detailed life analysis and timing.
