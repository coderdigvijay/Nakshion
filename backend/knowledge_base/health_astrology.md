# Health & Medical Astrology — Comprehensive Reference

## Body Parts Ruled by Each Sign

The zodiac maps onto the human body from head (Aries) to feet (Pisces), forming the cosmic anatomy.

**Aries:** Head, face, brain, eyes, upper jaw, skull, adrenal glands, blood pressure regulation. Prone to: headaches, migraines, sinus problems, fevers, head injuries, facial scars, hypertension, acute inflammatory conditions.

**Taurus:** Throat, neck, thyroid gland, vocal cords, tonsils, lower jaw, cervical vertebrae, ears (inner). Prone to: sore throats, thyroid disorders, neck stiffness, tonsillitis, vocal strain, jaw tension (TMJ), ear infections.

**Gemini:** Lungs, arms, hands, fingers, shoulders, nervous system (peripheral), bronchial system. Prone to: respiratory issues, asthma, bronchitis, carpal tunnel, arm/hand injuries, nervous anxiety, restlessness, insomnia.

**Cancer:** Stomach, breasts, chest wall, uterus, digestive system (upper), mucous membranes, body fluids, pancreas. Prone to: stomach ulcers, acid reflux, breast issues, emotional eating, water retention, digestive problems related to stress.

**Leo:** Heart, upper back, spine (thoracic), circulatory system, aorta, eyes (vitality). Prone to: heart disease, upper back pain, cardiovascular issues, blood pressure problems, spinal issues, eye problems, sunburn.

**Virgo:** Intestines (small and large), abdomen, digestive system (lower), spleen, nervous system (enteric/gut-brain axis). Prone to: IBS, intestinal disorders, food sensitivities, anxiety-related gut issues, OCD, eating disorders, hypochondria.

**Libra:** Kidneys, adrenal glands (cortex), lower back, skin, urinary system, acid-alkaline balance, hormonal balance. Prone to: kidney problems, lower back pain, skin conditions, hormonal imbalance, urinary tract infections, adrenal fatigue.

**Scorpio:** Reproductive organs, colon, rectum, prostate, bladder, sexual organs, elimination system, toxin processing. Prone to: reproductive issues, STIs, colon problems, hemorrhoids, prostate issues, urinary infections, toxin buildup.

**Sagittarius:** Hips, thighs, liver, sciatic nerve, arterial system, pelvis. Prone to: hip problems, sciatic pain, liver disease (especially from excess), thigh injuries, sports injuries, weight gain in hips/thighs.

**Capricorn:** Bones, joints (especially knees), skeletal system, teeth, skin (structure), hair, nails. Prone to: arthritis, knee problems, bone fractures, dental issues, dry skin, joint stiffness, osteoporosis, depression.

**Aquarius:** Ankles, calves, shins, circulatory system (peripheral), nervous system (electrical), varicose veins. Prone to: ankle sprains, varicose veins, circulatory problems, nervous disorders, shin splints, irregular heartbeat.

**Pisces:** Feet, toes, lymphatic system, immune system, pineal gland, mucus production. Prone to: foot problems (plantar fasciitis, bunions), lymphatic congestion, immune weakness, allergies, addictions, sleep disorders, psychosomatic illness.

## Preventive Guidance by Chart Analysis

### Reading Health in the Natal Chart

**Step 1:** Examine the 6th house (sign, ruler, planets) for daily health patterns.
**Step 2:** Examine the Ascendant and its ruler for constitutional type.
**Step 3:** Check aspects to the Sun (vitality) and Moon (emotional health).
**Step 4:** Identify the most stressed planets (heavily aspected, in detriment or fall) — these represent health vulnerabilities.
**Step 5:** Note the element balance:
- Fire deficiency: low energy, poor circulation, weak immune response
- Earth deficiency: poor body awareness, metabolic issues, grounding problems
- Air deficiency: respiratory vulnerability, poor oxygenation, mental fog
- Water deficiency: emotional suppression manifesting as physical tension, dehydration, hormonal issues
- Fire excess: inflammation, fevers, hyperactivity
- Earth excess: sluggishness, weight gain, rigidity
- Air excess: anxiety, nervous exhaustion, inability to relax
- Water excess: water retention, emotional overwhelm, lymphatic congestion

**Step 6:** Check for critical health degrees and aspects:
- Mars-Saturn aspects: chronic tension, inflammation with restriction, accidents
- Sun-Neptune aspects: vitality confusion, immune weakness, medication sensitivity
- Moon-Pluto aspects: emotional intensity affecting physical health, reproductive concerns
- Saturn in the 6th: chronic conditions requiring long-term management
