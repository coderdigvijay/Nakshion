# Vimshottari Antardasha Combinations

## How to use this file

Every Mahadasha (major period) is divided into nine Antardashas (sub-periods), one per planet, always beginning with the Mahadasha lord itself and then following the fixed Vimshottari order: Ketu, Venus, Sun, Moon, Mars, Rahu, Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury. The sub-period lengths below are computed from the standard proportion (see `kb_dasha_vimshottari_foundations.md`).

**Important framing.** These lines describe *tendencies of a blend of two planetary principles*. The real flavour of any sub-period depends on the chart: which houses each planet rules, where it sits, its dignity and its relationship to the Moon and Lagna. A sub-period labelled "friction" here can be productive for a chart where the two planets are functional allies, and a "supportive" one can feel hard if either planet is badly placed. Always combine these lines with the chart facts supplied for that person. They are guidance for reflection, not verdicts.

## Friendship rule used

The Mahadasha lord's view of the sub-period lord is recorded as friend, neutral or enemy using Parashara's natural friendship table:

- **Sun:** friends Moon, Mars, Jupiter; enemies Venus, Saturn; Mercury neutral.
- **Moon:** friends Sun, Mercury; no enemies; others neutral.
- **Mars:** friends Sun, Moon, Jupiter; enemy Mercury; Venus and Saturn neutral.
- **Mercury:** friends Sun, Venus; enemy Moon; others neutral.
- **Jupiter:** friends Sun, Moon, Mars; enemies Mercury, Venus; Saturn neutral.
- **Venus:** friends Mercury, Saturn; enemies Sun, Moon; others neutral.
- **Saturn:** friends Mercury, Venus; enemies Sun, Moon, Mars; Jupiter neutral.
- **Rahu and Ketu:** convention only (see school notes).

Friendship is not symmetric in this table (for example the Sun regards Mercury as neutral, while Mercury regards the Sun as a friend), so Sun-Mercury and Mercury-Sun read slightly differently.

## Reading hint: sub-period lord relative to the Mahadasha lord

Brihat Jataka (Varahamihira) already ties sub-period results to the sub-lord's position counted from the dasha lord (same sign, 5th/9th, 7th, 4th/8th). Many practitioners still check this: a sub-lord in 1, 5, 9 from the dasha lord tends to harmonise; 6, 8, 12 positions tend to create friction or need for adjustment. Treat this as a second-level refinement.

|---|---|---|

## Pratyantardasha (sub-sub-period) reminder

Each Antardasha splits again into nine Pratyantardashas using the same proportion: Pratyantar length = Antardasha length x (sub-sub lord's years / 120). Because these last days to a few weeks, they are used for fine timing only, and are best read together with transits. Keep conclusions soft: a Pratyantar is a short-lived colour on top of the Antardasha, not an independent verdict.
