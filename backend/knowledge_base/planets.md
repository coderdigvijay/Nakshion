# The Planets — Comprehensive Astrological Reference

## The Sun

### Core Archetype & Function
The Sun represents the conscious ego, identity, vitality, life purpose, and creative self-expression. It is the central organizing principle of the psyche — what you are becoming, your essential nature, and your will to exist.

### Psychological Representation
The Sun is the Hero's Journey in the birth chart. It represents the father archetype, authority, and the process of individuation (becoming who you truly are). A well-aspected Sun indicates a person who knows themselves and expresses their identity with confidence. A challenged Sun suggests identity crises, struggles with authority, or difficulty owning one's power.

### Dignity Table
| Dignity | Sign |
|---------|------|
| Domicile | Leo |
| Exaltation | Aries (19°) |
| Detriment | Aquarius |
| Fall | Libra |

### Sun Through the Signs (Brief)
- **Aries:** Bold, pioneering, competitive identity
- **Taurus:** Grounded, sensual, stability-seeking identity
- **Gemini:** Curious, communicative, dual-natured identity
- **Cancer:** Nurturing, protective, emotionally rooted identity
- **Leo:** Creative, radiant, self-expressive identity
- **Virgo:** Analytical, service-oriented, perfectionist identity
- **Libra:** Partnership-oriented, aesthetic, diplomatic identity
- **Scorpio:** Intense, transformative, power-seeking identity
- **Sagittarius:** Philosophical, adventurous, truth-seeking identity
- **Capricorn:** Ambitious, disciplined, legacy-building identity
- **Aquarius:** Innovative, humanitarian, contrarian identity
- **Pisces:** Empathetic, mystical, boundary-dissolving identity

### Retrograde
The Sun never retrogrades.

### Transit Effects
Sun transits last approximately one day per degree, one month per sign. When the transiting Sun conjuncts a natal planet, it illuminates and activates that planet's themes for about three days. The Solar Return (Sun returning to its natal position) marks the astrological birthday and sets the tone for the year ahead.

### Speed & Orbital Period
Approximately 1° per day. Completes the zodiac in one year (365.25 days).

## The Moon

### Core Archetype & Function
The Moon represents the emotional body, instinctual reactions, habits, memory, the unconscious mind, and the need for security. It is how you feel before you think — your automatic emotional response system.

### Psychological Representation
The Moon is the mother archetype, the inner child, and the emotional foundation. It governs what you need to feel safe, how you nurture others, and your relationship with your own vulnerability. The Moon sign is often more accurate for describing someone's day-to-day personality than the Sun sign, especially in private or under stress.

### Dignity Table
| Dignity | Sign |
|---------|------|
| Domicile | Cancer |
| Exaltation | Taurus (3°) |
| Detriment | Capricorn |
| Fall | Scorpio |

### Moon Through the Signs (Brief)
- **Aries:** Quick emotional reactions, needs independence, dislikes vulnerability
- **Taurus:** Steady emotions, needs comfort and routine, sensual nurturance
- **Gemini:** Processes feelings through talking, needs mental stimulation, emotionally restless
- **Cancer:** Deep emotional sensitivity, needs home and family, nurtures instinctively (domicile)
- **Leo:** Dramatic emotions, needs recognition and warmth, generous nurturance
- **Virgo:** Analyzes feelings, needs order and usefulness, worries
- **Libra:** Needs harmony in environment, avoids emotional conflict, nurtures through partnership
- **Scorpio:** Intense, all-or-nothing emotions, needs psychological depth, guards vulnerability fiercely
- **Sagittarius:** Optimistic emotional nature, needs freedom and adventure, avoids heavy feelings
- **Capricorn:** Controlled emotions, needs achievement and structure, difficulty with vulnerability
- **Aquarius:** Detached processing, needs intellectual freedom, uncomfortable with emotional intensity
- **Pisces:** Absorbs others' emotions, needs spiritual connection, boundaryless empathy

### Retrograde
The Moon never retrogrades.

### Transit Effects
The Moon changes signs every 2.5 days and completes the zodiac monthly. Lunar transits over natal planets activate emotional themes for a few hours. New Moons (conjunction with Sun) are initiation points; Full Moons (opposition to Sun) are culmination and release points.

### Speed & Orbital Period
Approximately 12–14° per day. Completes the zodiac in 27.3 days (sidereal month) or 29.5 days (synodic month, New Moon to New Moon).

## Mercury

### Core Archetype & Function
Mercury represents the mind, communication, perception, reasoning, learning style, and how information is processed and transmitted. It is the translator between the inner world and the outer world.

### Psychological Representation
Mercury is the Trickster/Messenger archetype. It governs how you think (analytical vs intuitive), how you communicate (verbose vs concise), and how you learn (visual, verbal, kinesthetic). Mercury is amoral — it serves whatever sign it occupies. A strong Mercury indicates verbal fluency and mental agility; a challenged Mercury suggests communication difficulties or deceptive thinking.

### Dignity Table
| Dignity | Sign |
|---------|------|
| Domicile | Gemini, Virgo |
| Exaltation | Virgo (15°) |
| Detriment | Sagittarius, Pisces |
| Fall | Pisces |

### Mercury Through the Signs (Brief)
- **Aries:** Quick, impulsive thinking, direct speech, argues to win
- **Taurus:** Slow, methodical thinking, practical communication, stubborn opinions
- **Gemini:** Rapid, versatile thinking, witty speech, information collector (domicile)
- **Cancer:** Emotional reasoning, remembers everything, communicates through feeling
- **Leo:** Dramatic expression, creative thinking, speaks with authority
- **Virgo:** Analytical, precise, detail-oriented, critical communication (domicile + exaltation)
- **Libra:** Diplomatic thinking, weighs all sides, indecisive but fair
- **Scorpio:** Penetrating, investigative thinking, strategic communication, detects lies
- **Sagittarius:** Big-picture thinking, blunt speech, philosophical but imprecise (detriment)
- **Capricorn:** Structured, strategic thinking, authoritative communication, plans long-term
- **Aquarius:** Innovative, unconventional thinking, detached reasoning, future-oriented
- **Pisces:** Intuitive, imaginative thinking, poetic but vague communication (detriment + fall)

### Retrograde
Mercury retrogrades approximately 3 times per year for about 3 weeks each time. Mercury retrograde is the most well-known astrological event in popular culture. Actual effects: communication breakdowns, technology glitches, travel delays, revisiting old conversations or decisions. Best use: review, revise, reconnect — not for initiating new contracts, purchases, or launches. The shadow period (pre and post retrograde) extends effects by about a week on each side. Orb of influence is strongest within 5° of stationing.

### Transit Effects
Mercury transits move quickly (about 1 sign per 2–3 weeks, except during retrograde). Mercury conjunct natal planets activates mental processes related to that planet. Mercury return (every year) refreshes communication style.

### Speed & Orbital Period
Approximately 1.5° per day (varies significantly — can be as slow as 0° when stationing). Completes the zodiac in approximately 88 days, but apparent period from Earth is about 1 year due to retrogrades.

## Venus

### Core Archetype & Function
Venus represents love, beauty, values, attraction, money, pleasure, aesthetics, and social grace. It governs what you find beautiful, how you attract and relate to others, and what you value enough to spend time and money on.

### Psychological Representation
Venus is the Anima archetype (in Jungian terms) — the feminine principle of receptivity, attraction, and relating. It governs self-worth, artistic sensibility, and the capacity for pleasure. A well-placed Venus indicates natural charm and a healthy relationship with pleasure. A challenged Venus suggests difficulties in love, self-worth issues, or materialistic excess.

### Dignity Table
| Dignity | Sign |
|---------|------|
| Domicile | Taurus, Libra |
| Exaltation | Pisces (27°) |
| Detriment | Scorpio, Aries |
| Fall | Virgo |

### Venus Through the Signs (Brief)
- **Aries:** Impulsive in love, attracted to confidence, competitive flirtation
- **Taurus:** Sensual, loyal, attracted to stability and physical beauty (domicile)
- **Gemini:** Flirtatious, attracted to wit, needs verbal connection in love
- **Cancer:** Nurturing love style, attracted to emotional security, domestic romance
- **Leo:** Dramatic, generous, attracted to glamour, love as performance
- **Virgo:** Reserved, shows love through service, critical of self and partner (fall)
- **Libra:** Romantic ideal, attracted to beauty and harmony, partnership-oriented (domicile)
- **Scorpio:** Intense, all-or-nothing love, attracted to depth and mystery (detriment)
- **Sagittarius:** Freedom-loving, attracted to adventure, love through shared philosophy
- **Capricorn:** Reserved, attracted to ambition and status, love as long-term investment
- **Aquarius:** Unconventional love, attracted to uniqueness, needs intellectual freedom
- **Pisces:** Idealistic, self-sacrificing, attracted to the wounded and artistic (exaltation)

### Retrograde
Venus retrogrades approximately every 18 months for about 40 days. Venus retrograde brings re-evaluation of relationships, values, finances, and aesthetics. Ex-partners often resurface. Not ideal for starting new relationships, getting cosmetic procedures, or making major purchases. Best use: reassessing what and whom you truly value.

### Transit Effects
Venus transits last about 1 day per degree, approximately 1 month per sign (except during retrograde periods which can extend to 4 months in one sign). Venus conjunct natal planets brings ease, pleasure, and attraction to those themes. Venus return (approximately every year) refreshes love and value themes.

### Speed & Orbital Period
Approximately 1° per day. Completes the zodiac in approximately 225 days, but apparent period from Earth is about 1 year.

## Mars

### Core Archetype & Function
Mars represents action, desire, aggression, sexuality, courage, competition, anger, and physical energy. It is how you assert yourself, fight for what you want, and express anger.

### Psychological Representation
Mars is the Warrior archetype and the Animus (masculine principle). It governs the survival instinct, the sex drive, and the capacity for decisive action. A strong Mars indicates a person who knows what they want and goes after it. A challenged Mars suggests difficulty with anger (suppressed or explosive), passivity, or destructive aggression.

### Dignity Table
| Dignity | Sign |
|---------|------|
| Domicile | Aries, Scorpio |
| Exaltation | Capricorn (28°) |
| Detriment | Libra, Taurus |
| Fall | Cancer |

### Mars Through the Signs (Brief)
- **Aries:** Pure aggression, direct action, competitive fire, quick to anger and quick to forgive (domicile)
- **Taurus:** Slow to anger, immense endurance, stubborn force, physically strong (detriment)
- **Gemini:** Mental aggression, verbal sparring, scattered energy, multiple pursuits
- **Cancer:** Passive-aggressive, protective aggression, fights for family, moody energy (fall)
- **Leo:** Dramatic action, courageous, performs under pressure, pride-driven fights
- **Virgo:** Precise action, critical aggression, works with meticulous focus, health-driven exercise
- **Libra:** Avoids direct confrontation, fights for fairness, charming aggression, indecisive action (detriment)
- **Scorpio:** Strategic, relentless, fights to the death, sexual intensity, revenge-capable (domicile)
- **Capricorn:** Disciplined action, calculated aggression, works toward long-term goals, patient force (exaltation)
- **Aquarius:** Fights for causes, unconventional tactics, detached anger, rebellious action
- **Pisces:** Passive, indirect action, fights through avoidance or sacrifice, creative energy

### Retrograde
Mars retrogrades approximately every 2 years for about 2.5 months. Mars retrograde brings frustration, delays in projects, revisiting old conflicts, decreased physical energy, and sexual frustration. Not ideal for starting major initiatives, surgeries, or physical competitions. Best use: strategizing rather than acting, internal strength-building.

### Transit Effects
Mars transits last approximately 2 days per degree, about 6 weeks per sign. Mars conjunct natal planets activates energy, conflict, and desire related to that planet. The Mars return (every 2 years) marks a new cycle of assertion and action.

### Speed & Orbital Period
Approximately 0.5° per day. Completes the zodiac in approximately 687 days (about 1.88 years).

## Jupiter

### Core Archetype & Function
Jupiter represents expansion, growth, abundance, wisdom, optimism, luck, higher education, philosophy, travel, and faith. It is the principle of "more" — wherever Jupiter sits, it amplifies and expands.

### Psychological Representation
Jupiter is the Sage/Teacher archetype. It governs meaning-making, the search for truth, and the capacity for faith and optimism. Jupiter represents what gives your life meaning and where you experience the most natural growth. A well-placed Jupiter indicates generosity, wisdom, and good fortune. A challenged Jupiter suggests excess, arrogance, or blind faith.

### Jupiter Through the Signs (Brief)
- **Aries:** Expansion through initiative, lucky in competition, excess courage
- **Taurus:** Financial growth, expansion through material accumulation, generous with resources
- **Gemini:** Intellectual expansion, many interests, knowledge breadth over depth (detriment)
- **Cancer:** Emotional wisdom, nurturing abundance, family as source of meaning (exaltation)
- **Leo:** Creative expansion, generous spirit, dramatic faith, lucky in self-expression
- **Virgo:** Growth through service, analytical wisdom, practical improvement (detriment)
- **Libra:** Social expansion, growth through partnership, justice-seeking, diplomatic wisdom
- **Scorpio:** Deep transformation, psychological expansion, hidden resources, occult wisdom
- **Sagittarius:** Maximum expansion, philosophical seeking, travel, luck, teaching (domicile)
- **Capricorn:** Disciplined growth, ambitious expansion, corporate success (fall)
- **Aquarius:** Humanitarian vision, social reform, progressive philosophy, group expansion
- **Pisces:** Spiritual expansion, compassionate wisdom, mystical faith, creative abundance (domicile)

### Retrograde
Jupiter retrogrades approximately once per year for about 4 months. Jupiter retrograde turns expansion inward — internal philosophical growth, reassessing beliefs and values, spiritual deepening. Less about external luck, more about internal wisdom. Often brings delayed rewards that arrive after the retrograde ends.

### Transit Effects
Jupiter transits last approximately 1 year per sign. Jupiter conjunct natal planets brings expansion, opportunity, and optimism to those themes for several weeks. Jupiter return (every 12 years, ages 12, 24, 36, 48, 60, 72) marks major growth cycles and opportunities.

### Speed & Orbital Period
Approximately 0.08° per day (about 5 arcminutes). Completes the zodiac in approximately 11.86 years.

## Saturn

### Core Archetype & Function
Saturn represents structure, discipline, responsibility, limitation, time, karma, authority, maturity, and mastery through effort. It is the principle of restriction — wherever Saturn sits, it demands hard work, patience, and earned authority.

### Psychological Representation
Saturn is the Senex (Wise Elder) archetype and the internalized father/authority figure. It governs the superego, fear, guilt, and the process of maturation through challenge. Saturn represents your greatest lessons, deepest fears, and ultimate mastery. A well-integrated Saturn indicates discipline, responsibility, and hard-won wisdom. A challenged Saturn suggests fear, rigidity, depression, or authority issues.

### Dignity Table
| Dignity | Sign |
|---------|------|
| Domicile | Capricorn, Aquarius |
| Exaltation | Libra (21°) |
| Detriment | Cancer, Leo |
| Fall | Aries |

### Saturn Through the Signs (Brief)
- **Aries:** Lessons in patience and measured action, fear of asserting self (fall)
- **Taurus:** Lessons in financial security, fear of scarcity, mastery of resources
- **Gemini:** Lessons in focused thinking, fear of being unintelligent, disciplined communication
- **Cancer:** Lessons in emotional boundaries, fear of vulnerability, duty to family (detriment)
- **Leo:** Lessons in humble leadership, fear of being unspecial, disciplined creativity (detriment)
- **Virgo:** Lessons in managing perfectionism, fear of imperfection, health discipline
- **Libra:** Lessons in relationship commitment, structured partnerships, fair authority (exaltation)
- **Scorpio:** Lessons in power and control, fear of powerlessness, disciplined transformation
- **Sagittarius:** Lessons in focused belief, fear of meaninglessness, structured philosophy
- **Capricorn:** Maximum discipline, career mastery, fear of failure, earned authority (domicile)
- **Aquarius:** Lessons in social responsibility, structured innovation, fear of conformity (domicile)
- **Pisces:** Lessons in spiritual discipline, fear of dissolution, structured compassion

### Retrograde
Saturn retrogrades approximately once per year for about 4.5 months. Saturn retrograde internalizes the lessons — reviewing commitments, confronting fears without external triggers, reassessing career and life structures. Karma accelerates during Saturn retrograde for those avoiding their lessons.

### Transit Effects
Saturn transits last approximately 2.5 years per sign. Saturn conjunct natal planets brings restriction, testing, and maturation to those themes for several months. The Saturn Return (ages 28–30, 57–59, 86–88) is the most significant transit in personal astrology — a complete restructuring of adult identity.

### Speed & Orbital Period
Approximately 0.03° per day (about 2 arcminutes). Completes the zodiac in approximately 29.46 years.

## Uranus

### Core Archetype & Function
Uranus represents revolution, innovation, liberation, disruption, eccentricity, technology, and sudden change. It is the principle of awakening — wherever Uranus acts, it shatters stagnation and demands authentic freedom.

### Psychological Representation
Uranus is the Rebel/Prometheus archetype. It governs individuation through disruption, the need to break free from conditioning, and the lightning-bolt moments that change everything. A well-integrated Uranus indicates originality, humanitarian vision, and progressive thinking. A challenged Uranus suggests chronic instability, perverse rebellion, or alienation.

### Dignity Table
| Dignity | Sign |
|---------|------|
| Domicile | Aquarius |
| Exaltation | Scorpio (debated) |
| Detriment | Leo |
| Fall | Taurus (debated) |

### Retrograde
Uranus retrogrades approximately once per year for about 5 months. Uranus retrograde internalizes the revolution — questioning personal freedom, reassessing where you've been inauthentic, inner disruption of old mental patterns. Less dramatic external events, more internal awakening.

### Transit Effects
Uranus transits last approximately 7 years per sign (generational influence). Uranus conjunct natal planets brings sudden change, liberation, and disruption to those themes — can last 1–2 years with multiple exact hits. The Uranus opposition (age ~42) is the astrological midlife crisis — a demand for authentic living.

### Speed & Orbital Period
Approximately 0.01° per day. Completes the zodiac in approximately 84 years.

## Neptune

### Core Archetype & Function
Neptune represents dreams, illusion, spirituality, imagination, compassion, dissolution, mysticism, and transcendence. It is the principle of dissolution — wherever Neptune touches, boundaries dissolve between real and imagined, self and other, mundane and divine.

### Psychological Representation
Neptune is the Mystic/Artist archetype. It governs the collective unconscious, spiritual longing, creative inspiration, and the dissolution of ego boundaries. A well-integrated Neptune indicates artistic genius, spiritual depth, and compassionate wisdom. A challenged Neptune suggests delusion, addiction, escapism, victimhood, or deception.

### Dignity Table
| Dignity | Sign |
|---------|------|
| Domicile | Pisces |
| Exaltation | Cancer or Leo (debated) |
| Detriment | Virgo |
| Fall | Capricorn or Aquarius (debated) |

### Retrograde
Neptune retrogrades approximately once per year for about 5 months. Neptune retrograde lifts the fog slightly — moments of clarity about where you've been deluding yourself, spiritual insights, creative breakthroughs that come from stripping away illusion.

### Transit Effects
Neptune transits last approximately 14 years per sign (generational). Neptune conjunct natal planets brings dissolution, confusion, spiritual awakening, and creative inspiration to those themes — can last 2–3 years. Often feels like losing ground before finding deeper truth.

### Speed & Orbital Period
Approximately 0.006° per day. Completes the zodiac in approximately 165 years.

## Pluto

### Core Archetype & Function
Pluto represents transformation, death and rebirth, power, the unconscious, obsession, regeneration, and the soul's evolution. It is the principle of total transformation — wherever Pluto acts, something must die for something greater to be born.

### Psychological Representation
Pluto is the Shaman/Phoenix archetype. It governs the deepest unconscious material — shadow work, trauma, compulsion, and the regenerative process. Pluto represents where you must face your darkest truth and emerge transformed. A well-integrated Pluto indicates psychological depth, transformative power, and resilience. A challenged Pluto suggests power struggles, manipulation, obsession, or destructive compulsions.

### Dignity Table
| Dignity | Sign |
|---------|------|
| Domicile | Scorpio |
| Exaltation | Aries or Leo (debated) |
| Detriment | Taurus |
| Fall | Libra or Aquarius (debated) |

### Retrograde
Pluto retrogrades approximately once per year for about 5–6 months. Pluto retrograde turns transformation inward — deep psychological processing, confronting power dynamics, releasing control. The most intense inner work happens during Pluto retrograde.

### Transit Effects
Pluto transits last approximately 12–31 years per sign (varies due to elliptical orbit, shortest in Scorpio, longest in Taurus). Pluto conjunct natal planets is the most transformative transit in astrology — can last 2–3 years with multiple exact hits. Forces death and rebirth in the areas governed by that planet.

### Speed & Orbital Period
Approximately 0.003° per day (varies significantly). Completes the zodiac in approximately 248 years.

## The North Node (Rahu)

### Core Archetype & Function
The North Node represents the soul's evolutionary direction, unfamiliar territory, growth edge, destiny, and the lessons this life is designed to teach. It is not a physical body but a mathematical point — the intersection where the Moon's orbital plane crosses the ecliptic heading northward.

### Psychological Representation
The North Node is the Growth Edge archetype. It points toward uncomfortable but essential growth. The sign and house of the North Node indicate where you need to stretch beyond your comfort zone. Following the North Node feels wrong at first but leads to fulfillment. Ignoring it leads to stagnation despite apparent success.

### Transit Effects
The North Node takes approximately 18.6 years to complete the zodiac, spending about 1.5 years per sign (moving backward through the zodiac — retrograde is its natural motion). The Nodal Return (ages 18–19, 37–38, 55–56) marks major destiny activation points. Eclipses occur near the nodal axis and are the most powerful catalysts for fate.

### Retrograde
The Nodes are almost always retrograde (moving backward through the zodiac). Brief periods of direct motion occur but are not astrologically significant in the same way planetary retrogrades are.

## The South Node (Ketu)

### Core Archetype & Function
The South Node represents past life gifts, innate talents, comfort zone, karmic baggage, and what must be released. It is always exactly opposite the North Node. The South Node shows where you've already mastered skills but now risk stagnation by over-relying on them.

### Psychological Representation
The South Node is the Past Life/Comfort Zone archetype. It indicates natural talents that came with you into this life but can become a crutch. Over-identification with the South Node leads to repeating old patterns. The key is to use South Node gifts in service of North Node growth, not as a substitute for it.

### Transit Effects
Same orbital dynamics as the North Node (always opposite). Transits to the South Node bring release, letting go, and sometimes loss that is ultimately liberating.

## Chiron

### Core Archetype & Function
Chiron represents the Wounded Healer — the deepest wound that, when acknowledged and integrated, becomes the source of your greatest healing gift to others. Discovered in 1977, orbiting between Saturn and Uranus, Chiron bridges the personal and transpersonal planets.

### Psychological Representation
Chiron is the Wounded Healer archetype (from the centaur in Greek mythology who could heal all wounds except his own). It governs core wounds, shamanic initiation through suffering, and the paradox of healing. Chiron's house and sign show where you carry a wound that cannot be fully "cured" but can be alchemized into wisdom and teaching.

### Dignity Table
Chiron is most commonly associated with Virgo and Sagittarius as potential domiciles, though this remains debated among astrologers.

### Retrograde
Chiron retrogrades approximately once per year for about 5 months. Chiron retrograde internalizes the healing process — old wounds resurface not to punish but to deepen understanding and integration.

### Transit Effects
Chiron takes approximately 50 years to complete the zodiac (very irregular orbit — spends 1.5 to 8 years in a sign). The Chiron Return (age 49–51) is a profound healing crisis and initiation into the "wounded healer" role — the passage into the second half of life.

### Speed & Orbital Period
Highly variable due to elliptical orbit. Approximately 50.7 years to complete the zodiac. Spends the longest in Aries/Taurus (about 8 years) and shortest in Libra/Scorpio (about 1.5 years).
