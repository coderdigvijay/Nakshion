# Functional Nature by Lagna

A planet's *natural* nature (Jupiter and Venus benefic, Saturn and Mars malefic) is overlaid by its *functional* nature, decided by the houses it rules for a particular ascendant. The same planet is helpful for one lagna and demanding for another. This page lists, for each lagna, the functional picture as a tendency, so a reading can say "Saturn here carries the 9th and 10th house, so it supports career and purpose" rather than "Saturn is bad".

## The rules in brief

- **Trikona lords** (1, 5, 9) are the most auspicious; lagna lord is both kendra and trikona.
- **Kendra lords** (1, 4, 7, 10) are neutral by default; natural benefics ruling kendras lose some benefic quality (kendradhipati dosha), natural malefics ruling kendras lose some malefic quality.
- **Dusthana lords** (6, 8, 12) and the 3rd and 11th lords are functionally challenging. The 8th is the strongest dusthana, except that the Sun and Moon do not suffer 8th lordship in the same way.
- **Maraka lords** (2nd and 7th) are associated with endings and health caution in a dasha-timing sense; read as pressure points, not predictions.
- **Yogakaraka**: a single planet ruling both a kendra and a trikona. Strongly benefic for that lagna.
- A planet ruling a trikona *and* a dusthana (for example Mars for Sagittarius: 5th and 12th) gives mixed results, with the trikona usually dominating.
- The Sun and Moon rule one sign only; they take the nature of the house they rule.
- Rahu and Ketu give results by their dispositor and placement.

## Aries (Mesha) lagna

Lagna lord Mars (1, 8). Functional benefics: Sun (5th), Jupiter (9th, 12th), Mars as lagna lord. Functional malefics: Mercury (3rd, 6th), Saturn (10th, 11th), Venus (2nd, 7th - maraka). Moon (4th) is mild. Yogakaraka: none; Sun-Jupiter and Mars-Jupiter relationships are the usual support.

## Taurus (Vrishabha) lagna

Lagna lord Venus (1, 6). Functional benefics: Saturn (9th, 10th, yogakaraka), Mercury (2nd, 5th), Sun (4th, neutral-good). Functional malefics: Jupiter (8th, 11th), Moon (3rd), Mars (7th, 12th). Yogakaraka: Saturn.

## Gemini (Mithuna) lagna

Lagna lord Mercury (1, 4). Functional benefics: Venus (5th, 12th), Saturn (8th, 9th: the 9th generally dominates). Functional malefics: Mars (6th, 11th), Sun (3rd). Jupiter (7th, 10th) is a kendradhipati and gives mixed results [debated: some call it neutral, some malefic]. Moon (2nd) neutral. Yogakaraka: none; Venus and Saturn give the best support.

## Cancer (Karka) lagna

Lagna lord Moon. Functional benefics: Mars (5th, 10th, yogakaraka), Jupiter (6th, 9th: the 9th dominates), Moon. Functional malefics: Venus (4th, 11th), Saturn (7th, 8th), Mercury (3rd, 12th). Sun (2nd) is neutral. Yogakaraka: Mars.

## Leo (Simha) lagna

Lagna lord Sun. Functional benefics: Mars (4th, 9th, yogakaraka), Jupiter (5th, 8th), Sun. Functional malefics: Venus (3rd, 10th), Saturn (6th, 7th), Mercury (2nd, 11th: neutral-to-malefic). Moon (12th) neutral. Yogakaraka: Mars.

## Virgo (Kanya) lagna

Lagna lord Mercury (1, 10). Functional benefics: Venus (2nd, 9th), Mercury. Functional malefics: Mars (3rd, 8th), Sun (12th), Moon (11th). Saturn (5th, 6th) is mixed; Jupiter (4th, 7th) is a kendradhipati with mixed results. Yogakaraka: none strictly; Venus is the most helpful.

## Libra (Tula) lagna

Lagna lord Venus (1, 8). Functional benefics: Saturn (4th, 5th, yogakaraka), Mercury (9th, 12th), Moon (10th). Functional malefics: Jupiter (3rd, 6th), Sun (11th), Mars (2nd, 7th - maraka). Yogakaraka: Saturn.

## Scorpio (Vrishchika) lagna

Lagna lord Mars (1, 6). Functional benefics: Moon (9th), Sun (10th), Jupiter (2nd, 5th). Functional malefics: Mercury (8th, 11th), Venus (7th, 12th). Saturn (3rd, 4th) neutral. Yogakaraka: none single; the Sun-Moon pairing (10th-9th lords) is the classic kendra-trikona link.

## Sagittarius (Dhanus) lagna

Lagna lord Jupiter (1, 4). Functional benefics: Sun (9th), Mars (5th, 12th: the 5th dominates), Jupiter. Functional malefics: Venus (6th, 11th), Saturn (2nd, 3rd). Moon (8th) is neutral-mild, since the Moon is exempt from the usual 8th lordship harshness; Mercury (7th, 10th) mixed. Yogakaraka: none single.

## Capricorn (Makara) lagna

Lagna lord Saturn (1, 2). Functional benefics: Venus (5th, 10th, yogakaraka), Mercury (6th, 9th: the 9th dominates). Functional malefics: Jupiter (3rd, 12th), Sun (8th), Mars (4th, 11th mixed). Moon (7th) neutral. Yogakaraka: Venus.

## Aquarius (Kumbha) lagna

Lagna lord Saturn (1, 12). Functional benefics: Venus (4th, 9th, yogakaraka), Mercury (5th, 8th: mixed). Functional malefics: Jupiter (2nd, 11th), Sun (7th), Moon (6th), Mars (3rd, 10th) mixed. Yogakaraka: Venus.

## Pisces (Meena) lagna

Lagna lord Jupiter (1, 10). Functional benefics: Moon (5th), Mars (2nd, 9th), Jupiter. Functional malefics: Saturn (11th, 12th), Venus (3rd, 8th), Sun (6th). Mercury (4th, 7th) mixed (kendradhipati). Yogakaraka: Mars is sometimes named because it rules the 9th and 2nd, but it does not hold a kendra, so strict definitions do not call it yogakaraka [debated].

## Using this in the app

The engine (`functional_nature` in `vedic.py`) scores each planet's house lordships with a simplified rule (trikona +2, kendra +1 for natural malefics or -1 for natural benefics, 3rd/11th -1, 6th -2, 8th -2 unless the planet also rules the lagna, 2nd and 12th 0) and calls a planet a yogakaraka if it rules a kendra (4, 7, 10) and a trikona (5, 9). Its benefic/malefic lists can therefore differ from the traditional lists above on borderline cases (for instance Mercury for Gemini or Saturn for Gemini). When the two disagree, report the engine's list as computed and mention that texts differ.

The engine's `Raja Yoga` and `Viparita Raja Yoga` rely on these lordships. When the lagna is unknown none of this applies: lordships depend on house numbering from the lagna. An explanation based on the Sun or Moon sign alone can describe a planet's general nature but not its functional role (see `kb_chart_nakshatra_pada_and_ascendant_notes.md`).
