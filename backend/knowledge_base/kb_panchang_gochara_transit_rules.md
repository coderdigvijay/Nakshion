# Gochara: Transits Read from the Natal Moon

**Gochara** ("movement through") is the Vedic method of reading current planetary positions against the natal chart. Its distinguishing rule: transits are counted from the **natal Moon sign (Chandra Lagna / Janma Rashi)**, not from the Ascendant or Sun. The planet's present sign is counted as house 1 = the Moon's sign, 2 = the next sign, and so on. Gochara is read together with the running dasha, which is considered the main timing frame. The Phaladeepika (ch. 26), Brihat Samhita (ch. 104) and Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra all give versions of the tables below.

Everything here describes **tendencies** in classical texts. A transit through an "unfavourable" house is a prompt to be careful or patient in that theme, not a forecast of a bad event.

## Favourable houses from the natal Moon (summary table)

| Planet | Traditionally favourable houses from Moon | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sun | 3, 6, 10, 11 | Phaladeepika lists 3, 6, 10 and treats the 11th as good for all planets |
| Moon | 1, 3, 6, 7, 10, 11 | Many panchangs use this set for Chandra Bala |
| Mars | 3, 6, 11 | |
| Mercury | 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 11 | |
| Jupiter | 2, 5, 7, 9, 11 | |
| Venus | 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 11, 12 | everything except the 6th, 7th and 10th |
| Saturn | 3, 6, 11 | |
| Rahu/Ketu | usually 3, 6, 10, 11 (Sun-like) | see school notes; sources vary |

**The 11th house** (gains) is the one position favourable to all grahas in the Phaladeepika. The 3rd, 6th and 11th are the classic "upachaya" (growth with time) places and make the most universally favourable group.

## Vedha: obstruction of a favourable transit

A planet transiting a favourable house can have its good results blocked if another planet is at the same moment in the matching **vedha** house (counted from the same Moon). Read each pair as "favourable house - blocking house":

- **Sun:** 3-9, 6-12, 10-4, 11-5
- **Moon:** 1-5, 3-9, 6-12, 7-2, 10-4, 11-8
- **Mars:** 3-12, 6-9, 11-5
- **Mercury:** 2-5, 4-3, 6-9, 8-1, 10-8, 11-12
- **Jupiter:** 2-12, 5-4, 7-3, 9-10, 11-8
- **Venus:** 1-8, 2-7, 3-1, 4-10, 5-9, 8-5, 9-11, 11-6, 12-3 (one source set differs in the order; this pairing agrees with the more consistent of the two tables consulted)
- **Saturn:** 3-12, 6-9, 11-5

**Exemptions:** The **Sun and Saturn do not obstruct each other** (father and son), and **Moon and Mercury do not obstruct each other** (Mercury is traditionally the Moon's "son"). Rahu/Ketu are generally outside the vedha scheme [school varies]. Some teachers also describe **vipareeta (reverse) vedha**, where a planet in a bad house is relieved by another in a counterpart house; sources differ and it is not given a fixed table here [unverified].

A related note: vedha is applied between planets that have the same "kind" of effect-giving function. Many modern readers weight vedha lightly and use it only as a modifier to an already favourable placement.

## Chandra Gochara: the Moon through the 12 houses

The Moon takes about 2.25 days per sign, so its effect is brief and describes the day's emotional and practical tone. Phaladeepika-style results, expressed in plain terms (wording in sources varies):

1. **1st (own Moon sign):** a good-humoured, nourished feeling; comfort and good food; Moon here is favourable.
2. **2nd:** expense and distraction; careful with money and speech; not favourable.
3. **3rd:** success in efforts, small gains, support from siblings and friends; favourable.
4. **4th:** restlessness, a sense of anxiety about home or comfort; not favourable.
5. **5th:** worries about children or decisions; mood dips; not favourable.
6. **6th:** relief from opponents and health issues; favourable.
7. **7th:** pleasant partnerships, meals and companionship; favourable.
8. **8th:** mental strain, a vulnerable day; traditionally the most cautious Moon position (the "Chandrashtama" day); take things slowly and avoid new commitments.
9. **9th:** obstacles and delays; not favourable.
10. **10th:** success in work, recognition; favourable.
11. **11th:** gains and fulfilment of wishes; favourable.
12. **12th:** expenses, wasted effort and loss of sleep; not favourable.

**Chandrashtama** (the Moon in the 8th from the natal Moon, i.e. about two and a quarter days each month) is the most commonly quoted caution, and almanac apps flag it. Treat it as a reminder to be gentle with oneself, not as a prediction.

## Sun (about one month per sign)

Favourable in the 3rd, 6th, 10th and 11th from the Moon: more confidence, support from authority, success against opposition. Cautious in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 5th, 7th, 8th, 9th and 12th: tiredness, friction with seniors or the father, pressure on health and travel, expenses (wording varies by source).

## Mars (about 45 days per sign)

Favourable in 3rd, 6th, 11th: energy, courage and gains through effort. In other houses the classical texts describe heat and impulsiveness: a tendency toward disputes, impulsive spending and tension in relationships. The sense is "more drive; more risk of friction", so channel it into physical activity and focused work.

## Mercury (about 1 month, with retrograde loops)

Favourable in 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 11: clear communication, gains in business and study. In 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 12 the tradition describes scattered thinking, quarrels and misunderstandings. Retrograde periods are an additional modern popular theme; the classical gochara tables do not treat retrogression specially.

## Jupiter (about one year per sign)

Favourable in 2, 5, 7, 9, 11: growth, family blessings, learning, children, good fortune. In the 1st, 3rd, 4th, 6th, 8th, 10th and 12th the classical texts describe obstacles, displacement, or setbacks, usually modest. Jupiter's transit is the primary yearly marker, for example for opportunities in marriage, children and education in some traditions.

## Venus (about one month per sign)

Favourable in all houses except 6, 7 and 10 (per the Phaladeepika summary): comfort, relationships, aesthetic pleasure. In 6, 7, 10 the tradition describes friction in relationships or a drain from indulgence.

## Saturn (about 2.5 years per sign)

Favourable in the 3rd, 6th and 11th: steady progress, discipline pays, help from the less privileged or dependents. In the other houses the tradition describes delays and pressure: in the 1st, 2nd and 12th from the Moon is the well-known **Sade Sati** (7.5 years total), when Saturn moves through the 12th, 1st and 2nd signs from the natal Moon; the 4th and 8th from the Moon are the **Dhaiya/Kantaka Shani** periods (about 2.5 years each). These are described as periods of maturation, demanding patience, structured work and honest self-assessment rather than disaster. Many people report constructive outcomes from them, so this base presents them as "periods that ask for steadiness".

## Rahu and Ketu (about 18 months per sign, retrograde)

Rahu and Ketu are conventionally given Saturn-like or Sun-like favourable positions (3, 6, 10, 11 are the common set); in other houses the texts describe confusion, unusual events and detours. Because Rahu is always 180 degrees from Ketu, the pair flips polarity. Their gochara is discussed in the context of the Moon only; classical sources and modern teachers differ on weighting, so give broad language ("themes of change and unpredictability") rather than specific results.

## Planets in houses 7 to 12 versus 1 to 6

Several teachers note that transits through houses 1 to 6 from the Moon tend to act more gently than those through houses 7 to 12 [secondary-source statement; medium-low confidence].

## Ashtakavarga-based refinement

**Ashtakavarga** adds a quantitative layer to gochara. For each planet there is a **Bhinnashtakavarga (BAV)** chart in which the planet's transit sign scores 0 to 8 **bindus** (benefic points) from the eight reference points (seven planets plus the Ascendant). The sum of all BAVs for each sign is the **Sarvashtakavarga (SAV)**, with 337 points in total across the twelve signs and an average of about 28 per sign.

Common rule-of-thumb refinements (modern teachers; classical texts say much of this but in verse form):

- A planet transiting a sign where its own BAV has **4 or more bindus** is considered to deliver positive results of that sign's theme; with 3 or fewer it is more demanding. For Jupiter and Saturn some teachers ask for 5 and 4 respectively.
- A sign or house with an SAV of **28 or more** is considered supportive; below 25 or so, less so.
- A transit through an "unfavourable" house with many bindus is considered milder; a transit through a "favourable" house with few bindus may deliver less.
- The "double transit" idea (Jupiter and Saturn both influencing the same house) is popularly used for timing events and combined with the running dasha.

These thresholds are heuristics, not a single canonical rule; sources give slightly different cut-offs.

## How to use gochara in a conversation

Count houses from the **natal Moon sign** (and say so), list each slow planet's current house from the Moon, mention whether the placement is in the traditional favourable set and whether vedha could modify it, then add context from the dasha and the Ashtakavarga bindus if available. Use phrases like "traditionally read as" and "a period that invites". Never state outcomes with certainty, and never substitute a gochara reading for professional advice in health, legal, or financial matters.
