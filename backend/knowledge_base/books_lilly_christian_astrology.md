# William Lilly's Christian Astrology — Key Teachings Synthesized

## Overview

William Lilly (1602-1681) was England's most celebrated astrologer. His magnum opus "Christian Astrology" (1647) remains the most comprehensive horary astrology textbook ever written. It codified the rules of horary judgment that practitioners still use today. The work spans three volumes: the first covering astronomical basics and planetary natures, the second devoted entirely to horary astrology, and the third to natal astrology.

---

## Horary Astrology Principles

Horary astrology answers specific questions based on the chart cast for the moment the question is understood by the astrologer. Lilly's system rests on several foundational principles:

### The Moment of Understanding

The horary chart is erected not for when the querent first thinks of the question, but for when the astrologer receives and comprehends it. Lilly was meticulous about recording the exact time. The chart must be "radical" — fit for judgment — before proceeding. Signs of radicality include the Ascendant ruler matching the querent's temperament, or the Moon's last aspect reflecting recent events the querent describes.

### Considerations Before Judgment

Lilly outlined several conditions that may render a chart unfit for judgment:

- **Saturn in the 7th house**: Traditionally warns the astrologer may err. In practice, this consideration is weighed but not treated as absolute.
- **Late degrees rising (27-30)**: Suggests the matter is already decided or the question comes too late.
- **Early degrees rising (0-3)**: The matter is premature; insufficient information exists.
- **Moon void of course**: The Moon makes no further applying aspects before leaving its sign. Generally indicates "nothing will come of the matter," though Lilly noted exceptions when the Moon is in Cancer, Taurus, Sagittarius, or Pisces.
- **Via Combusta (15 Libra to 15 Scorpio)**: Moon traveling through these degrees was considered unfortunate for judgment.

### Significators

Every horary question assigns significators:

- **The querent** is represented by the 1st house cusp lord, the Moon, and planets in the 1st house.
- **The quesited** (person or thing asked about) is assigned the house corresponding to the question's topic.
- **The Moon** is always a co-significator of the querent and acts as a general timer and transmitter of light.

## Essential Dignities Scoring System

Lilly used a precise numerical scoring system to assess a planet's strength. This "dignity score" determines whether a significator can deliver results.

### Dignity Table

| Dignity | Score |
|---|---|
| Domicile (own sign) | +5 |
| Exaltation | +4 |
| Triplicity ruler | +3 |
| Term (bound) ruler | +2 |
| Face (decanate) ruler | +1 |
| Detriment (opposite domicile) | -5 |
| Fall (opposite exaltation) | -4 |
| Peregrine (no essential dignity) | -5 |

A planet scoring +5 or more is essentially strong and can act effectively. A planet at -5 or worse is debilitated and struggles to produce favorable outcomes. A peregrine planet — one with no essential dignity whatsoever — was considered by Lilly as "like a stranger with no resources," often indicating a person acting dishonestly or without standing.

### Triplicity Rulers

Lilly used the traditional triplicity system:

- **Fire signs (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius)**: Sun by day, Jupiter by night, Saturn participating
- **Earth signs (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn)**: Venus by day, Moon by night, Mars participating
- **Air signs (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius)**: Saturn by day, Mercury by night, Jupiter participating
- **Water signs (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces)**: Mars by day, Venus by night (some controversy here), Moon participating

### Terms (Bounds)

Each sign is divided into five unequal segments, each ruled by a different planet (excluding the luminaries). Lilly primarily used the Egyptian term system rather than the Ptolemaic. For example, in Aries: Jupiter rules 0-6 degrees, Venus 6-12, Mercury 12-20, Mars 20-25, Saturn 25-30.

### Faces (Decanates)

Each sign divides into three 10-degree faces following the Chaldean order: Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon, Saturn, Jupiter — cycling continuously from Aries 0 degrees.

## Accidental Dignities

Beyond essential dignity, Lilly catalogued accidental factors affecting planetary strength:

### Strengthening Factors

- **Angular placement** (houses 1, 10, 7, 4): +5 for 1st/10th, +4 for 7th, +3 for 4th
- **Succedent houses** (2, 5, 8, 11): +2 to +3
- **Direct motion**: +4
- **Swift in motion** (faster than average daily motion): +2
- **Oriental of Sun** (for Saturn, Jupiter, Mars): +2
- **Occidental of Sun** (for Venus, Mercury, Moon): +2
- **Free from combustion**: significant bonus
- **Cazimi** (within 17 minutes of the Sun): +5, treated as enormously empowered
- **Moon increasing in light**: +2
- **Conjunct favorable fixed stars** (Regulus, Spica, etc.): +5 or +6

### Weakening Factors

- **Cadent houses** (3, 6, 9, 12): -5 for 12th, -4 for others
- **Retrograde**: -5
- **Combust** (within 8.5 degrees of Sun): -5
- **Under the Sunbeams** (within 17 degrees of Sun): -4
- **Slow in motion**: -2
- **Besieged** (between Mars and Saturn with no benefic aspect): severe debility
- **Conjunct malefic fixed stars** (Algol, Scheat): -5

## Planetary Hours

Lilly used planetary hours extensively in horary judgment. Each day is divided into 12 daytime hours and 12 nighttime hours, each ruled by a planet following the Chaldean order.

### Calculation Method

1. Determine sunrise and sunset for the location and date.
2. Divide the daytime period (sunrise to sunset) by 12 to get the length of one daytime planetary hour.
3. Divide the nighttime period (sunset to next sunrise) by 12 for nighttime hours.
4. The first hour of each day is ruled by the planet governing that day: Sunday = Sun, Monday = Moon, Tuesday = Mars, Wednesday = Mercury, Thursday = Jupiter, Friday = Venus, Saturday = Saturn.
5. Subsequent hours follow the Chaldean order: Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon.

### Radicality Check

If the planetary hour ruler matches the Ascendant ruler or the triplicity rulers of the Ascendant sign, Lilly considered this supporting evidence of the chart's radicality.

## Reception: Mutual and Mixed

Reception describes the relationship between two planets based on the dignities they occupy.

### Mutual Reception

Two planets are in mutual reception when each occupies a dignity of the other. The strongest form is mutual reception by domicile — for example, Mars in Cancer and Moon in Aries. Each planet is in the other's home sign. This acts as a powerful connection, almost as if the planets can "exchange places," significantly mitigating debility.

### Mixed Reception

Mixed reception occurs when the relationship is asymmetric in dignity type. For example, Planet A is in Planet B's domicile, but Planet B is only in Planet A's exaltation or term. The planet receiving the higher dignity has the advantage in the relationship.

### Reception in Horary Application

When the significator of the querent applies to aspect the significator of the quesited, and there is reception between them, the matter is much more likely to succeed. Reception indicates willingness, welcome, and mutual benefit. Without reception, even a perfecting aspect may indicate a reluctant or unsatisfying outcome.

## Antiscia and Parallels

### Antiscia

Antiscia are mirror points across the Cancer-Capricorn axis (the solstice axis). A planet at 10 degrees Gemini has its antiscion at 20 degrees Cancer (since Gemini-Cancer mirror across the solstice point). Planets on each other's antiscia points act as a hidden conjunction — a secret connection or covert influence.

### Antiscia Pairs by Sign

| Sign Pair | Mirror Axis |
|---|---|
| Aries / Virgo | |
| Taurus / Leo | |
| Gemini / Cancer | |
| Libra / Pisces | |
| Scorpio / Aquarius | |
| Sagittarius / Capricorn | |

### Contra-Antiscia

These are mirror points across the Aries-Libra axis (equinoctial axis). Generally considered less powerful than antiscia, and sometimes interpreted as more antagonistic or challenging hidden connections.

### Practical Use

Lilly checked antiscia when no obvious applying aspect existed between significators. An antiscion contact could indicate a hidden path to resolution, or a secret party involved in the matter.

## Key Rules for Horary Judgment

### Perfection of the Matter

A horary question is answered "yes" when the significators of the querent and quesited come together through:

1. **Applying conjunction or aspect**: The faster planet applies to aspect the slower within the orb appropriate to both planets.
2. **Translation of light**: A third planet separates from one significator and applies to the other, carrying the light (and the matter) between them. This often represents a go-between or intermediary.
3. **Collection of light**: Both significators apply to a third, slower planet, which "collects" their light. This indicates a third party who brings the matter together.
4. **Mutual application**: Both significators move toward each other (one direct, one retrograde, for instance).

### Frustration and Prohibition

- **Frustration**: Before the significator perfects an aspect, it goes retrograde or the receiving planet changes sign, preventing completion.
- **Prohibition**: A third planet interposes an aspect to one of the significators before perfection, blocking the outcome.
- **Refranation**: An applying planet turns retrograde before perfecting the aspect.

### Timing

Lilly timed events using the degrees between the applying significator and the aspect point:

- **Cardinal signs** suggest days or weeks
- **Fixed signs** suggest months or "fixed" longer periods
- **Mutable signs** suggest weeks or months
- **Angular houses** speed the timing
- **Cadent houses** delay the timing

The actual unit depends on the nature of the question and the signs/houses involved. Experience and context guide the astrologer.

## House Meanings in Horary Context

Lilly's house significations in horary were extremely precise:

| House | Primary Significations |
|---|---|
| 1st | The querent, their body, temperament, life force |
| 2nd | Movable wealth, personal finances, possessions |
| 3rd | Siblings, neighbors, short journeys, letters, rumors |
| 4th | Father, land, real estate, buried treasure, end of the matter |
| 5th | Children, pregnancy, pleasure, gambling, creativity |
| 6th | Illness, servants, employees, small animals, daily labor |
| 7th | Marriage partner, business partners, open enemies, the astrologer, any "other person" |
| 8th | Death, inheritance, partner's money, fear, surgery, occult |
| 9th | Long journeys, higher education, religion, law, dreams, divination |
| 10th | Career, reputation, mother (some traditions), authority, judge |
| 11th | Friends, hopes, wishes, groups, the king's treasury |
| 12th | Hidden enemies, imprisonment, self-undoing, large animals, secrets, sorrow |

### Turned Houses

A critical Lilly technique: when asking about a specific person, "turn" the houses so that person's significator house becomes their 1st house. If asking about a sibling's finances, the 3rd house is the sibling (their 1st), so the 4th house (2nd from 3rd) represents their money.

## Common Horary Question Types

### "Will I Get the Job?" (10th House Questions)

The querent is signified by the 1st house ruler and Moon. The job is signified by the 10th house ruler. If the significators apply to a major aspect (conjunction, trine, sextile, or even square), the querent receives the job. Reception between the significators indicates the employer wants the querent (or vice versa). The Moon applying to the 10th house ruler is often sufficient alone. If the 10th ruler is retrograde, the job may fall through or the employer may change their mind. If a 6th house planet (the competition, other candidates) interposes an aspect before perfection, another candidate gets the position.

### "Will the Relationship Work?" (7th House Questions)

The querent is the 1st house ruler; the partner is the 7th house ruler. Application between the two significators with mutual reception is ideal — it shows both parties want the relationship and are willing to work for it. Venus (natural significator of love) and the Moon's condition provide supplementary evidence. The 5th house describes the pleasure in the relationship; the 8th house describes sexual compatibility. If the 7th ruler is in the 12th house, the partner may have hidden issues or secret enemies. If the 7th ruler is retrograde, the partner may return to a former lover.

### "Where Is the Lost Object?" (2nd House for Possessions)

For lost objects, the 2nd house (personal possessions) or the 4th house (things in the home) provides the significator. The sign on the relevant house cusp indicates the direction and type of location:
- Fire signs: Near heat sources, fireplaces, walls, or high places
- Earth signs: On the ground, in gardens, basements, or low dark places
- Air signs: On shelves, bookcases, upper rooms, or near windows
- Water signs: Near water, bathrooms, kitchens, or in damp places

If the significator is in a cadent house, the object is hard to find. If angular, it is nearby and will be found easily. A retrograde significator often means the object returns on its own.

### "Should I Travel?" (9th House for Long Journeys, 3rd for Short)

The 9th house ruler represents the journey's quality and outcome. Benefics (Jupiter, Venus) in the 9th or aspecting the 9th ruler favor travel. Malefics (Saturn, Mars) there or afflicting the ruler warn of delays, danger, or disappointment. The condition of the 9th ruler in terms of essential dignity indicates whether the destination will meet expectations. A peregrine 9th ruler suggests the journey is not what it appears.

### "Will I Recover from Illness?" (Medical Horary)

The 1st house represents the patient. The 6th house represents the illness. The 10th house represents the doctor. The 4th house represents the outcome (end of the matter). If the 1st ruler separates from the 6th ruler, the patient separates from the illness — recovery is indicated. If the 1st ruler applies to the 6th ruler, the illness worsens. Moon translating light from benefics to the 1st house aids recovery. Saturn or Mars in the 1st house without reception from the 1st ruler is dangerous.

## The Part of Fortune and Arabic Parts

Lilly used Arabic Parts (Lots) extensively. The Part of Fortune is the most important:

**Part of Fortune = Ascendant + Moon - Sun** (day charts)
**Part of Fortune = Ascendant + Sun - Moon** (night charts, though some use the same formula regardless)

The Part of Fortune in horary indicates where the querent's material fortune lies. Its house placement shows the source of gain. Its ruler's condition shows whether that fortune materializes. In natal work, Lilly used the Part of Fortune to assess overall material prosperity.

Other Parts Lilly employed:
- **Part of Marriage:** Ascendant + Descendant - Venus
- **Part of Death:** Ascendant + 8th cusp - Moon
- **Part of Commerce:** Ascendant + Mercury - Sun
- **Part of Inheritance:** Ascendant + Saturn - Moon

Each Part derives from a formula involving the Ascendant and two other chart points. The resulting degree is interpreted like a sensitive point — planets aspecting it or its ruler's condition reveal the state of that life area.

## Lilly's Approach to Fixed Stars in Horary

Lilly paid attention to fixed stars when they fell on the Ascendant or significant house cusps, or when they closely conjoined a significator planet:

**Fortunate stars:**
- **Regulus (29 Leo in Lilly's era):** Military honor, glory, success — but with potential sudden fall from grace
- **Spica (23 Libra):** Wealth, preferment, protection, artistic talent
- **Fomalhaut (3 Pisces):** Fame through idealism, but requires maintaining integrity

**Unfortunate stars:**
- **Algol (25 Taurus):** Violence, loss, misfortune — the most feared fixed star. A significator conjunct Algol in a horary warns of extreme difficulty.
- **Vindemiatrix (9 Libra):** Falseness, widowhood, depression
- **South Scale (14 Scorpio):** Disease, unscrupulousness

Lilly used fixed stars as confirming evidence rather than primary factors. A horary judgment should not rest on a fixed star alone, but a benefic star on a significator strengthens a positive answer, and a malefic star on a significator adds gravity to a negative one.

## Practical Application for AI Guidance

When providing horary-style guidance:

1. **Never claim to cast actual horary charts** — the moment of question and precise time are required for genuine horary work.
2. **Use Lilly's dignity system** to explain planetary strength in natal charts. A user's chart ruler in domicile versus detriment dramatically changes the interpretation.
3. **Apply reception concepts** to relationship questions — mutual reception between Venus and Mars significators indicates genuine mutual attraction.
4. **Reference void-of-course Moon** in timing guidance — suggest waiting when the Moon is void for important new initiatives.
5. **Use the house meanings** to ground interpretations in concrete life areas.
6. **Timing methods** from Lilly can supplement transit-based timing in natal work.

Lilly's system remains the gold standard for precise astrological technique because it provides clear, testable rules rather than vague intuitions. His emphasis on structured judgment — checking dignities, receptions, aspects, and house placements systematically — produces reliable, consistent interpretations.
